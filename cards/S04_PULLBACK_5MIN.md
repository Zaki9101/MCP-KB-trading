Setup: 5-minute uptrend pullback.
Conditions: price above the 9 and 20 EMA and VWAP, near the high of day. Daily above the prior two highs. First higher-high bar within 2 to 3 five-minute bars, with L2 confirmation (R15).
Entry: on the higher-high bar with L2 confirmation.
Stop: pullback low (inside the cap, R03).
Exit signals: pullback reaches the 9 EMA; signal expires after 3 bars (R13).
Refuse if: below the 9 or 20 EMA or VWAP (PASS, name S04). Signal older than 3 bars (PASS, name S04).
