Setup: jump base entry (JBE) base breakout, 4 base shapes.
Conditions: a jump of at least 1.5x the base (ideally 2x). Base 25 cents or less, at its high. No sharp pullback. Volume dry in the base, then rising on the break. Index not against. Tier 1 risk.
Entry: on the break with the ask thinning, or on a held retest. Never inside the base.
Stop: below the base low, inside the cap (R03).
Exit signals: base over 25 cents; a spike at the upper line; a rush to the upper line. Exit on a vertical bar with a volume peak (R13).
Refuse if: base over 25 cents, no jump, no L2 confirmation (R15). Over-cap stop (R03).
