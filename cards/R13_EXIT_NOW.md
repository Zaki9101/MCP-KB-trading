Rule: EXIT now if any of these is true:
- The level the trade is built on fails.
- The tape turns: bid weakens, sellers join the offer, spreads widen, bids thin.
- A volume peak forms after an extended leg.
- News turns against the trade.
- An offer 30 cents ahead of price.
- A 5-minute close under the 9 EMA: sell half (half exit, not full).
- Session close (flat before the close, R02).
Management: any of the above -> EXIT (or the half-exit for the 9 EMA case), name R13. None -> HOLD.
