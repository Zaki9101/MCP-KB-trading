Rule: a 1-to-5-minute momentum entry needs level 2 or time-and-sales confirmation. No confirmation, no trade. Never enter on a vertical bar; enter only on a base.
Refuse if: no L2 or tape confirmation (PASS, name R15). Entry on a vertical bar (PASS, name R15).
