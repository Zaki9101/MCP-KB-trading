Rule: the stop is a structure stop, padded 1 to 3 cents beyond the level, or 5 to 10 cents beyond a whole or half dollar. Cent cap: 10 cents on stocks $1 to $8; 20 cents on any stock until the size-up gates. The stop distance is measured in cents from entry.
Over the cap: SKIP. Cutting shares does not make an over-cap stop legal, because the cap limits distance, not dollars. The stop is never widened to fit the structure.
Refuse if: stop distance is above the cap (PASS, name R03). Stop at or above entry for a long (PASS, name R03).
