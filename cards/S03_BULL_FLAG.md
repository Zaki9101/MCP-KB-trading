Setup: bull flag or ABCD. Move of +15 to 20% on news or low float, then a pullback.
Conditions: pullback 50% or less of the move, on small low-volume candles. Flag high breaks with L2 confirmation (R15). Above VWAP.
Entry: on the flag-high break with L2 confirmation.
Stop: flag low (inside the cap, R03).
Exit signals: below VWAP; no L2 signal; no catalyst (R13).
Refuse if: pullback over 50%; no L2; below VWAP (PASS, name S03 and the failed rule).
