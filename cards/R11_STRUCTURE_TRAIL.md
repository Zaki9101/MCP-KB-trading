Rule: after breakeven, trail the stop under the newest structure: step low, channel line, last higher low, or a Fibonacci level with real support. A trail under a level with no support gets hit by noise.
Management: the stop moves up only, never down. If the trail level is not clearly defined, answer HOLD and name the missing level (INSUFFICIENT_DATA if the chart cannot be read).
