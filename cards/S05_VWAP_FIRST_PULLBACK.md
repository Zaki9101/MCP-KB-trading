Setup: first pullback after a VWAP break.
Conditions: a heavy candle closes above VWAP with rising EMAs. A 1-to-3-candle pullback holds. Break of the pullback high with L2 confirmation (R15).
Entry: on the break of the pullback high.
Stop: pullback low (inside the cap, R03). Book example: entry 25.98, stop 25.80 (labelled 19 cents, arithmetic 18 cents), inside the 20-cent cap for a stock above $8 (BP p.190).
Exit signals: close back below VWAP (R13).
Refuse if: close back below VWAP; no pullback hold; no L2 (PASS, name S05 and the failed rule).
