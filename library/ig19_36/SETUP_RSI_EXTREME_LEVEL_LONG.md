Conditions: 5-min RSI under 10 at a support, plus doji/hammer, then a bullish doji.
Entry: Long on a new 1- or 5-min high after the hold. Tier1.
Stop: Tighter of box or prior-candle low, in the cap.
Target and exits: Half at 9 EMA or 2R, then VWAP; new 5-min low = out. Bank 25-50%, stop to breakeven.
Time: After the hold, never bar 1. Not 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: Band touch or RSI alone (alert only); level absent; stop over 20c.
