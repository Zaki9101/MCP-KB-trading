Conditions: Named open pattern, Tier1 size, spread 5c or less, range 25c or less (and under ATR), chase 3c or less.
Entry: Entries only 9:35-10:00; 5-min range default from 9:35. 1-min range entry is withdrawn if it falls inside the no-entry window.
Stop: 10c stop cap ($1-$8), 20c any stock.
Target and exits: First target 2.0R, bank 25-50%.
Time: No entry 9:20-9:35 (watch only). 9:35-9:50 open break, 9:50-10:00 drive. 15-min range: mid/large caps enter 9:45; 30-min: 10:00.
Refuse if: Low float, index against, or first-minute volume under 100K shares.
