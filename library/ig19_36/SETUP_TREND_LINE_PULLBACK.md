Conditions: Rising trend line; reversal bar at the line (overshoot counts; best is the 2nd failed try).
Entry: Buy stop 1 tick above the reversal bar.
Stop: Beyond the pullback or overshoot extreme, inside the cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c any stock).
Target and exits: Test of the old extreme; trail under pullbacks.
Time: No entries 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00 ET; first lot max 300 sh
Refuse if: Every touch of a line; a break without a test.
