Conditions: All six pivot strategies need: L2 imbalance 5,000+ shares on the side expected to hold the pivot; good volume (premarket ~500k ideal, or RVOL above 100%); spike volume 50-100% above average daily volume for B/C; daily check of open and close pivot vs prior high/low/close.
Entry: Per strategy.
Stop: Cent caps still apply; never widen.
Target and exits: D/E/F smallest size.
Time: 1-min chart.
Refuse if: No L2 imbalance or no volume = no trade.
