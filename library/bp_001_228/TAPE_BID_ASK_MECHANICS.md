Conditions: Take the book at face value: bid = buyers, ask = sellers. When the ask gets bought, price moves up; when the bid gets filled, price moves down. Keep one fixed Level 2 window of 10 visible levels, colour the best four tiers and watch only the top two.
Entry: Judge a big print at the ask by the price response after it (buyer lifting or seller absorbed).
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Guessing hidden intent behind a bid instead of reading the response.
