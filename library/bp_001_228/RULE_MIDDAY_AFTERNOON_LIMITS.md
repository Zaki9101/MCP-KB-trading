Conditions: No new entries 11:00-13:00. After 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size, and only if your own hour-of-day statistics show an edge. TR's afternoon JBE and cup examples are not to be traded as written until you have that record. The notes' setups-by-time map ranks setups; it is not a licence to trade them.
Entry: Past 10:00 use the 5-min chart for momentum unless there is breaking news or extremely heavy volume.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Flat before the close.
Time: 11:00-13:00 ET no new entries; after 13:00 restricted.
Refuse if: No journal evidence of an edge in that hour. Thin midday stocks; overtrading levels.
