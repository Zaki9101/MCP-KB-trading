Conditions: Prior-day high: if price is not above the previous days' highs, do not touch the stock. Premarket high: a break and trade above it at the open is a strong signal. HOD is probably the most important level. A level is an area (about +/-$0.02 at $1.15). Broken resistance should hold as support; win rate drops if price retraces to the original support.
Entry: A print above a level is not a breakout: time above it plus volume confirm. Whole-number trap: let the first 10-20c go, then go long above the real level.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Two or more upper wicks closing below the premarket high (move away until it stops trading there). Long near important long-term resistance (pass until cleared). Premarket breakout on very low volume.
