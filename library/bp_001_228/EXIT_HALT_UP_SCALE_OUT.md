Conditions: You are long and the stock halts up (first or second halt up only).
Entry: n/a.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: After the resume sell 50%; watch L2 and the tape; sell the rest at the next major level (for example the 200 MA). CEP example ladder: 15% at +$0.04, 15% of the rest at +$0.08, 50% of the rest at the level; usually leave 10% to run.
Time: First minutes after the resume.
Refuse if: Holding into a third or later halt.
