Conditions: 5-min passes the four criteria; leading gapper or RVOL stock only. L2: bids at nearly every level, thin ask with a gap above. Last two 5-min candles with good volume and higher lows. Pullback at most 50%, small low-volume candles.
Entry: Two red candles to the 1-min 9 EMA get bought up fast: T = break of the 1-min HOD; G = break of the previous candle's high (the safer entries). L2 must confirm (bids at every level, prints speeding on the break).
Stop: At the held level (book example $5.40 for a $5.47 entry, about $0.07).
Target and exits: Book example $5.47 to $6.06, sold in portions; usually leave 10% to run, sell all in a channel; massive ask wall = close the whole position.
Time: Premarket: smaller size as warm-up.
Refuse if: Mid-channel entry; chart break without L2; no bids under the entry.
