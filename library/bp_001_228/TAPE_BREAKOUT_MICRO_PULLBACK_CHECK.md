Conditions: Watch the micro pullback right after a break of HOD or any level: a higher low confirms the breakout; a lower high warns of a false breakout.
Entry: Re-enter only on the higher-low version.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Exit at once if already in and the bid starts collapsing fast (book example 96, 75, 49, 21, 4) with red prints speeding up.
Time: Seconds after the break.
Refuse if: Lower high after the break. A fake push: the ask jumps a level but the bid does not follow and price snaps back.
