Conditions: No absolute block size. Set the Time & Sales filter per stock and time of day so about 5-10 prints a day stand out. Starting tiers: unfiltered (overall colour and speed); 1,000+ shares (removes algo prints; default for breakouts and pullbacks); 2,000-3,000+ shares (large orders at key levels). 5,000 shares is only a stand-in for a liquid $5-$20 momentum name.
Entry: After a huge print, follow the direction of the immediate ticks.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Classifying an order as real or fake by its share count.
