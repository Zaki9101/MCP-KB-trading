Conditions: Classify the lead-in first: a volume spike is neither a buy nor a sell by itself. Real break: steady volume before, price grinding or basing under the level, spike as resistance is consumed, next bars hold above it on lower volume (editor test).
Entry: Buy the break only in the real-break case; otherwise wait for a pullback to the broken level on dry volume.
Stop: Under the old resistance or base.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Vertical run into resistance with volume growing bar by bar and the largest bars at the high. Heavy volume already at the level, then a spike on the break.
