Conditions: Trade only the first or second attempt at a level: the first is strongest, the second weaker, the third or later usually a trap.
Entry: First attempt: normal Tier1 entry. Second attempt only after a new pullback forms a new higher low that is re-confirmed on Level 2 (ask shrinking again, bid stepping up again), no larger than half the first lot.
Stop: Every new try: new signal, new stop.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: Two tries per level per day.
Refuse if: Third or later attempt at the same level. Revenge re-entry on the same push.
