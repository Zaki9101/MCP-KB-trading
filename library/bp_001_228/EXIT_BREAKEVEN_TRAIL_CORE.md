Conditions: After the first partial.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: Stop on the remainder to breakeven, then trail by structure: under each new support the break creates (TR Ex17: stop to 30.00 once 30.25 broke), under the 9 EMA, or under the last pullback low.
Target and exits: Hold a core only with a stop at the breakout level; never hold full size for the 'big' move. Keep a small piece until stopped out. Price back at entry: exit at breakeven.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Trailing on a level with no real support.
