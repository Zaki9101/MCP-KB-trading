Conditions: Write an if-then roadmap per stock: trigger, stop, conditions that favour and invalidate the setup, and the action per index scenario. Think 'if the market does X, I do Y'; no direction predictions. Draw at most 3 lines: HOD plus the two closest levels (premarket high or prior-day high first, then the move's pivot). Watch whole and half dollars without drawing them.
Entry: Set alerts 1 cent above a tight level and re-evaluate when they fire.
Stop: Stop level broken before the trigger = setup void.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Night before and 9:00-9:20 ET.
Refuse if: No written plan for the stock. Price not above the prior-day high (do not touch).
