Conditions: Premarket trading needs 500,000+ premarket shares. Premarket has no halts and moves are not clean. 8:00-9:00: at most a couple of easy trades at 1/2 to 1/4 size.
Entry: Half size or less.
Stop: Inside the cent caps.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: Before 9:20 ET; nothing 9:20-9:35.
Refuse if: Premarket volume under 500k. Halts or unclean moves.
