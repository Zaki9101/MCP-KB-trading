Conditions: Long at first resistance with increased volume, or price action turning erratic.
Entry: Approaching prior support on slow selling: do not buy it.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: At first resistance: choppy market exit fully; trending market exit partly and trail. Price dropping fast or volume skyrocketing (erratic): exit fully or scale out; steady volume with gradual drift your way: hold. Weak bid at a mini-range low: pull your bid.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Holding full size into erratic action.
