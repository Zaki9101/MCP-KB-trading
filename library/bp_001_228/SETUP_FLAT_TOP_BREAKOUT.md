Conditions: Ascending wedge (higher lows, no higher highs) under a whole- or half-dollar level that is also the high of day.
Entry: Buy the first candle that breaks the flat top: a half-size initial order at the apex on a surge of buying through Time & Sales with ask sellers being bought up; add only on confirmation (ask shrinking, bids stacking behind price).
Stop: Just under the ascending support line, inside the Ch.19 cap.
Target and exits: No fixed target; use the whole/half-dollar ladder and take profit into the spike.
Time: Main window.
Refuse if: Stuffing: heavy green prints but the ask will not shrink. The retest of the broken level fails to hold. Holding through a volume climax (flat-top breaks can round-trip fast).
