Conditions: Pullback entries are the default. A breakout entry (level, flag high, HOD, whole dollar) needs L2: ask eaten and bid stepping up together.
Entry: Aggressive near-support entry (near C, the 9 EMA hold) only if the market trends your way, the ask is thinning, the approach is gradual, size is Tier1 or half, stop under the structure. Apex or premarket-high buy only with a half-size first order, a tape surge, a first target of 2.0R or better, and no add until the stop can move to breakeven.
Stop: Under the structure (below C, ascending support or flag low), inside the cap.
Target and exits: First target 2.0R or better.
Time: Never for an opening-range break.
Refuse if: Mixed tape or unclear book.
