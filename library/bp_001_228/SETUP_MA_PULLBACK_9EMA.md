Conditions: Strong uptrend where price repeatedly dips 1-2 candles to a rising short MA (for example the 9 EMA) on falling volume without breaking it. After every fresh breakout, plan the breakout plus the first and second pullback; the best pullback is a bull flag touching the 9 EMA.
Entry: First green candle off the MA touch, or the break of the pullback candle's high; on the 1-min, only once price is back above its 20 EMA.
Stop: Below the flag low or the 9 EMA, inside the Ch.19 cap.
Target and exits: Hold while closes stay above the 9 EMA; exit when it breaks.
Time: Main window.
Refuse if: Entry far from the MA (wide stop). Stock that does not respect any MA.
