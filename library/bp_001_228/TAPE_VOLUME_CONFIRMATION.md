Conditions: For a long, volume rises as price rises and dries up on pullbacks. A forming candle that already has most of the prior candle's volume (book example: prior 100,000, forming 80,000 with 30 s left) = presume increasing.
Entry: At the break with rising volume; on a retest, want dry volume on the pullback and rising volume on the move away.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Pullback on heavy volume and a second leg without heavy volume: first target only. Volume rate jumps and the angle steepens: prepare to exit. Falling volume during a trend warns of a turn.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Breakout on weak volume.
