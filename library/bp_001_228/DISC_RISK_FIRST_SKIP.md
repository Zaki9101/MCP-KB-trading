Conditions: Professionals assess risk, amateurs assess potential. Judge stop size, liquidity and slippage before the upside.
Entry: Enter only when price and volume show a setup you recognise.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Not comfortable with the stop size, liquidity or slippage, however large the potential. The only reason is news, a story, 'value' or a prediction. You have to talk yourself into it.
