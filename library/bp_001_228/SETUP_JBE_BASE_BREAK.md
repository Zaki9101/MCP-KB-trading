Conditions: Jump on a volume peak, then a base at the high: width 25c or less, jump at least 1.5x the base (ideally 2x), no sharp pullback, volume dry in the base. Shapes: flat line, range after a shallow pullback, narrow range, ascending triangle. Index favourable or neutral.
Entry: Tier1 on the break of base high U with rising volume and a thinning ask, or the held retest of U. Never inside the base.
Stop: Below base low L, inside the cap, else skip. Check you can exit at the stop.
Target and exits: Half at the first price/volume peak, stop to breakeven, trail under each new support; first target 2.0R.
Time: Main window; after 13:00 only reduced.
Refuse if: Base over 25c; jump under 1.5x; price rushing to U; blow-off spike; very long base; L breaks before the trigger.
