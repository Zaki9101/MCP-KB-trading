Conditions: Heavy green prints hit the ask but the displayed ask size will not shrink, or price stays pinned (book examples: ask stuck at 23 while hundreds of green prints hit it; huge prints at 0.70 with price pinned). Stuffing = a hidden seller absorbing breakout buying right at resistance.
Entry: No long. Treat the price as resistance; wait for the ask to clear or the seller to vanish.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Already long: start exiting.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Buying or chasing a stuffed breakout.
