Conditions: Accumulation: slow steady rise, constant volume (smart money). Advance: volume steadily increasing, angle steepening. Frenzy: near-vertical price, volume peak (the crowd). Distribution: heavy volume, no progress. Shallow retracement: big volume up, shallow decreasing volume on the pullback = trend intact.
Entry: Buy the slow steady rise or the shallow low-volume pullback; confirmation is a move through the previous high. In the advance, add only on shallow low-volume pullbacks.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Frenzy: sell or scale out. Distribution: longs out.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Buying the frenzy stage or distribution.
