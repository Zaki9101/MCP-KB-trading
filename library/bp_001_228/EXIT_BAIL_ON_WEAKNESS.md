Conditions: Open long where the tape turns against the thesis.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: Do not wait for the hard stop.
Target and exits: Exit immediately on a burst of selling (run of red prints), a clear lack of the momentum the setup required, or a single massive new sell order. Mixed 'Christmas tree' prints with no colour dominance = momentum stalled: exit or adjust.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Waiting for the stop while the tape says the trade is wrong.
