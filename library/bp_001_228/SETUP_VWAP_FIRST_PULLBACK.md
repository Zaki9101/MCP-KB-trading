Conditions: Leading gapper or leading relative-volume stock. Price broke above VWAP on a strong, heavy-volume candle and trades above the rising 9 and 20 EMA.
Entry: Break of the high of the first pullback candle once the pullback (1-3 small candles, lighter volume) holds above VWAP and the 9 EMA; L2 and tape confirm, ask thins.
Stop: Not numbered in the source; editor note: below the pullback low (or below VWAP if the pullback reaches it), inside the cap.
Target and exits: First target must measure 2.0R; partials into strength; trail under the 9 EMA or the last pullback low.
Time: From 9:35; main window to 11:00.
Refuse if: Not a leading stock; pullback over 50% of the impulse or on heavy red volume; close back below VWAP; volume dries while price stalls under the prior high; buying the impulse candle; treating a later dip as a 'first' pullback.
