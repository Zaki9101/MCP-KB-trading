Conditions: Price at a known level in the right 1-min and 5-min area (HOD, premarket high, flag break, held support); a large ask sits at the key price.
Entry: Long when prints speed up green, the ask at the key price shrinks row by row (book example 78, 63, 28, 9 at 1.03) and the bid below holds (1.02). Busy stock: one green ask level left and shrinking = trigger. Speed proves fills, not cancels.
Stop: Under the bid that holds (book example 5.40 under an entry near 5.47).
Target and exits: Surge through the next levels expected; no number given.
Time: Inside the session windows.
Refuse if: Ask flat under green prints (hidden seller). Asks step up over static bids. At HOD without bid support. A big bid shrinking as red prints speed up.
