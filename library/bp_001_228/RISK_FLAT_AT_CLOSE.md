Conditions: Day trades are flat at the close; nothing is held overnight.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: An overnight gap has no stop.
Target and exits: Flat before the close.
Time: Close.
Refuse if: Holding a falling stock overnight on 'value' (value is not a timing tool).
