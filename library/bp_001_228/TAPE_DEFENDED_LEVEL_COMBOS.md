Conditions: Read size together with whether it gets hit. Large ask eaten by green prints and gone, price squeezes up = bullish continuation (same as the ask-shrinking entry at a wall). Large bid that holds unhit while buyers lift the ask elsewhere = strongest bullish reversal signal (institutions defending).
Entry: Trade with the squeeze, or the bounce off the defended bid, inside a qualified setup.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Size alone. A large bid hit repeatedly and gone (flush, bearish). A large ask that holds while sellers hit the bid (bearish).
