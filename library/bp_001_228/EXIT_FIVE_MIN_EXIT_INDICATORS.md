Conditions: Momentum long managed on the 5-min chart.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Exit everything if a 5-min candle makes a new low before half is sold, or price breaks below the 5-min 9 EMA. Exit flat if the breakeven stop is hit. Exception: with half sold and the stop at breakeven you may hold through a new 5-min low while price stays above entry.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Holding a full position through a new 5-min low.
