Conditions: Green = trade at the ask, red = at the bid; inside-spread prints show as yellow, white or grey by platform, so map your own colours. Speed matters more than colour or size. Green prints with price rising = active buying. Green prints with price flat = hidden seller (do not buy). Red prints with price flat = hidden buyer (long only as a held-bid trade).
Entry: A red-to-green flip with large blocks is a trigger only inside a qualified setup.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Mixed 'Christmas tree' prints while in a trade: exit or adjust now. Large green prints failing to lift price: exit an existing long.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Inside-spread prints alone (indecision). Trade speed slowing at key resistance (expect reversal, no breakout buy).
