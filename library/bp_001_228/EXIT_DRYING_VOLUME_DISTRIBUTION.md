Conditions: Slow advance on shrinking volume with a flat top (buying dries up above), or big volume with no progress at resistance (passive distribution).
Entry: No new longs above the level.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Exit longs; for the drying-volume top, exit on the first small flat candles.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Buying into distribution.
