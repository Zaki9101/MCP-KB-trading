Conditions: Near-vertical price bar with a volume peak, often at the end of a breakout or after an extended leg (book: a 5-min bar about 2x average volume).
Entry: No new buy into it; new entry only after the next level.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Sell all or half INTO the spike; do not wait for confirmation. After half is sold, if volume bars start to disappear, exit the rest. Buying dries up above the high: exit before the bid falls.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Buying the vertical bar.
