Conditions: Main window 9:35-11:00: trade 1-2 prepared stocks; throttle up only if the market is hot.
Entry: Only named setups inside the window.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Window over is a stop-the-day trigger: close everything.
Time: Stop at 11:00 or two hours after the first entry, whichever comes first. Extend to 11:30 only for a stock still moving clean (CAM sees volume to 11:30).
Refuse if: Staying on from boredom after the cutoff.
