Conditions: Do not buy the first thrust at the open blind: the first leg has unknown size and shake-out risk.
Entry: Take an open-drive entry only with pre-open structure already on your sheet (held after-hours range, tight premarket range breaking, bids stepping up, index above its resistance), from 9:35, Tier1. Otherwise wait for the first pullback or second leg.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: From 9:35 to about 10:00 for open-drive entries.
Refuse if: No bullish pre-open structure. After the tape's change of character. After about 10:00.
