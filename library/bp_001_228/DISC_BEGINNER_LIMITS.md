Conditions: Beginners: no fades, no halts, no scalps. Open-range breaks are for experienced traders only; beginners watch and record the open. A new trader takes only A-grade low-float names at Tier1 inside the stop caps.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Halt trading without experience. B-grade low floats as a new trader.
