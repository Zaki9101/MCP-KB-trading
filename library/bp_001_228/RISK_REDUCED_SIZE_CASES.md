Conditions: Cut size when the edge is weaker or exits are less certain: thin book (half); premarket (half or less); normal or choppy market (cut size and trade count); short interest above 20% up to 30% (reduce, experienced only); after 13:00 (reduced).
Entry: n/a.
Stop: Reduced size never means a wider stop.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Doubling size after losses.
