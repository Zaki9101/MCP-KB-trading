Conditions: Trade at most 2 stocks a day (3 in earnings season). Watch 3-5 candidates only to pick the better ones; the long list never exceeds 6. Ticket cap 20 a day.
Entry: A live account starts at one stock and one trade a day until the Ch.19 gates are passed. 10 trades a day is a simulator figure, not a live target.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Whole session.
Refuse if: No candidate passes the funnel: do not trade.
