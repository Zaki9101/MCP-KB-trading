Conditions: Daily chart for selection and context only (SMA 9/20/50/100/200, recent highs, long-term levels). 5-min to qualify the trend and find the flag; 1-min to find the entry price, never to qualify a stock. A 15-second chart is a timing aid only. Indicators lag; candles plus volume give the most real-time read.
Entry: Use the same time frames before every decision.
Stop: Never place a stop from the 15-second chart.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Switching indicators or time frames to cure under-performance.
