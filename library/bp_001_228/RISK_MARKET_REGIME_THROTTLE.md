Conditions: Momentum breakouts are regime-dependent. Hot market (movers easy to find): throttle up, hold winners. Cold or normal (choppy, fake breakouts): throttle down, smaller, shorter, sell sooner. Very low liquidity: smaller, fewer stocks, 10-minute timeout if angry.
Entry: Throttle up only after the market proves hot.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Cold market: sell sooner.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Momentum chasing in a normal, selectively volatile market.
