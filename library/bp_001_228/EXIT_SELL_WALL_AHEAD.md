Conditions: A large sell wall sits ahead of your long (book example 50,000 shares at resistance), or a massive ask wall appears at your target area.
Entry: n/a.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Do not wait for it to clear: sell into the bid early. At a massive ask wall (book example $7.10), close the whole position at once.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Holding into the wall hoping it clears.
