Conditions: A-B: strong push on volume (a catalyst helps). B-C: profit-taking pullback. C: support higher than A where price stops (VWAP, 9/20 EMA, a daily or premarket level).
Entry: Not at A, not at B, not at C itself. Enter after the hold: first higher low at C, then the break of the high of the last small pullback candle (new 1-min high, volume above the pullback candles).
Stop: Under C. If entry-to-stop exceeds the cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise), skip.
Target and exits: Sell half at D (targets: HOD at B, a daily level, the premarket high) and move the stop to entry; sell the rest at target or when sellers take control. A new 5-min low = exit all.
Time: Main window 9:35-11:00.
Refuse if: C-D volume not higher than the prior candles. Buying only at D. Near-C aggressive entry unless every Ch.19 aggressive gate holds.
