Conditions: A-grade needs fresh news today (after the prior close or before the open) of a strong type: improved margins, raised guidance, revenue far above consensus, new product or drug, FDA clearance or strong small-cap earnings. You must state it in one line. No news = B-grade at best, only with 1M+ volume, RVOL 2.0+, clean price action, reduced size and a stop at a level.
Entry: News picks the stock; the tape picks the entry. Never enter on news, a story or 'value' alone.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Premarket.
Refuse if: No news and no volume. Earnings alone with a small gap or light premarket volume. Buyout target. A gapper that faded from +30% to +10% premarket. Cheap biotech unless you know the story, every filter passes and it has worked for you.
