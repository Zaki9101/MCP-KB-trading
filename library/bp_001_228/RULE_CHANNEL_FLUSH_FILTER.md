Conditions: After a spike and a big pullback, price chopping between two lines drawn on the pullback pivots is a channel. F/A/B zones: F = previous candle high, A about 20% down, B about 35%; F-A zone safest, A-B only with strong L2, below B wait for price to return to F.
Entry: Wait for a break out of the channel, a surge, a pullback, then the standard L2 entry.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Inside the channel or while price breaks the pivot lines. A recent flush (a shooting star at a new HOD); check the last 10-30 minutes (Ch.19: 30). Asks rising while bids stay the same.
