Conditions: Offers that were lifting stop lifting and bids start to drop, or sellers defend a price for the first time (book examples: a 3.40 offer not lifted with bids dropping to 3.39; 107.40 lifted but 107.50 not).
Entry: n/a.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Hit the bids and lighten to Tier1, or get flat. A buying pattern that disappears near resistance: exit longs.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Staying full size after the change.
