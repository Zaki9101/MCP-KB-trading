Conditions: All must hold on the 5-min chart: price above the 9 EMA, the 20 EMA and VWAP, and close to high of day (about to break it, or on the first pullback after breaking it). Clean 1-min and 5-min action: large candles, short wicks, no gaps. Daily: above the previous two days' highs. Price also above 1-min VWAP.
Entry: Do not enter as a pullback starts: a candle with a lower low, a red close, or a green candle with a lower high.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Before every momentum entry.
Refuse if: Any criterion missing. Price below 1-min VWAP (massive weakness, do not touch). Pullback beyond the 5-min 9 EMA: trade again only if it returns near HOD or hits the scanner again.
