Conditions: Before any momentum entry: passes selection and the 5-min filter; above VWAP on 1-min and 5-min; price area agrees with a chart level (HOD, premarket high, pivot, flag break); no long-term resistance right above; L2 and tape confirm; stop price and dollar risk written; first target and partial rule decided; not mid-channel; not the third halt.
Entry: Base setups: base 25c or less, jump at least 1.5x the base, volume dry in the base.
Stop: Stop at the level that must hold, padded, inside the cap.
Target and exits: First target decided before the order.
Time: Before every entry.
Refuse if: A recent flush or 2+ upper wicks closing under the key level. Stage is vertical on a volume peak. A good book at the wrong price area.
