Conditions: Check the spread first when selecting. A-grade $0.01-$0.02; $0.03-$0.05 = B-grade; $0.05 is the hard limit. A 20-cent spread on a sub-$10 stock only if you already know that stock well and size down.
Entry: n/a (selection filter; the setup sheet and tape decide).

Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time; re-check before each entry.
Refuse if: Spread $0.06 or more: wait for it to shrink to $0.05 or less. Wide spread together with low volume, few levels or small sizes: skip, cut size or expect only a scalp.
