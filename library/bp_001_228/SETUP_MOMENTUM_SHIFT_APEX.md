Conditions: After a surge, number the 1-min pullback: (1) pullback low, (2) bounce high, (3) next pullback low, which must be higher than (1).
Entry: Break of (2) = point (4), the apex. Strongest when it is also the first 5-min candle to make a new high and the 5-min touched the 9 EMA during the pullback; act when 1-min and 5-min signal near the same price. First order half size.
Stop: not stated as a rule; examples sit below (3). Ch.19 caps apply.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: Main window 9:35-11:00.
Refuse if: Price takes out (1): the retracement is still running, no trade.
