Conditions: A missed first move costs nothing. Hesitate after six green candles in a row on a runner.
Entry: Missed the break: chase at most 3c. Do not revenge re-enter on the same push.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: Do not bank 10 cents when the plan was a couple of points (take a partial, keep the rest with a stop).
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: FOMO buying the top; averaging down; trying to catch the entire move.
