Conditions: Rim at the day high. Rounded cup of at least 30 minutes (ideal 1 hour+) back to the same high. Handle retraces at most 50% of the cup depth and lasts at most 30% of the cup time (25% desirable). Volume dry at the cup bottom and handle bottom, rising at the right rim and on the break.
Entry: Break above the rim line after the handle, on heavy volume.
Stop: Below the handle bottom, inside the Ch.19 cap.
Target and exits: not stated in pages 159-228 (sheet continues past p.228).
Time: Afternoon only after 13:00 as a proven setup at reduced size.
Refuse if: Cup under 30 minutes; handle over 50% of cup depth or over 30% of cup time; volume does not repeat the shape.
