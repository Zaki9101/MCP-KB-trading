Conditions: An inside day trades completely inside the previous day's high and low; there is no reason to expect momentum. Inside days often follow high-volatility days.
Entry: Treat it as a fresh candidate only after price breaks the prior day's range and follows through.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Premarket and intraday.
Refuse if: Stock trading inside the prior day's range.
