Conditions: 1-min chart: three green candles on high volume, then two red pullback candles on low volume.
Entry: The green candle that follows the two red candles.
Stop: Written resting stop at the structural low (the book's 1:2 mental stop is not adopted).
Target and exits: All out around 2R or at the first tape signal; 2.0R is a minimum, not a ceiling. Exit on four red candles in a row.
Time: Main window.
Refuse if: Volume pattern missing. After 3 losing trades in a row, stop trading.
