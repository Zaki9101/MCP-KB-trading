Conditions: A volatility pause follows a move of more than 10% in 5 minutes; assume at least 5 minutes with no exit, much longer on news. A halted stock can reopen far away, so a stop does not protect you. Warning signs on L2: ask pinned at the top with grey halt levels; LULD shown with a price. Verify halt rules and bands with your broker.
Entry: No new entries when halt signs appear. Trade only halts up, only the first and second.
Stop: Do not hold a size you could not take reopening 20% against you (editor note).
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time; premarket has no halts.
Refuse if: Beginner or not experienced. Third or later halt; halt down. Slow internet or a platform that needs a confirm click.
