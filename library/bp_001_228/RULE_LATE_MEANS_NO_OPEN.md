Conditions: Arrive 7:30 (8:00 latest) to watch premarket range, volume and news. Daily max loss and period stop are set at the broker before the open.
Entry: Late or unprepared before the open: do not trade the open; trade the demo until 11:00. Starting after 9:00 = no trades until the open.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Arrival 7:30-8:00 ET.
Refuse if: Arriving after 8:00 if you want the open. Forcing trades to catch up.
