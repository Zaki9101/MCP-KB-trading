Conditions: Name the day type before choosing setups. Trend (index and stock make steady higher highs and higher lows): aggressive breakouts and pullbacks with the trend, scale out, trail. Narrow range: wait for the break. Expanding range (new high, falls through the low, bounces, repeats): cut activity, stay out.
Entry: Trending market = aggressive; unstable = conservative.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Trading breaks on an expanding-range day. After a series of losses do not change the setup list; change approach only when the market's character changes.
