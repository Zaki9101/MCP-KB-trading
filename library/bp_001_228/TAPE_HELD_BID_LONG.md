Conditions: A buyer takes 5-10x the expected size on the bid and the bid does not drop (book example: 5k usually prints before the bid drops; 50k prints and it holds). Passive accumulation (big volume, no decline at support) marks a level only; it does not lift price by itself.
Entry: Step in front of the held bid at starter size: a defined-risk entry, not a forecast.
Stop: Just under the held level; exit the moment the bid drops.
Target and exits: Take the first defined target and do not add until price lifts off the level and volume expands on the advance. Support breaks = close all longs.
Time: Inside the session windows.
Refuse if: The same volume at a held offer or at resistance (distribution). Big bids that decrement and rebid while you hit them: get out at once.
