Conditions: Daily prep: key SPY levels (QQQ for tech; NDX or a sector index for sector movers), market trend, what moves the market today and any scheduled number. Set alerts at 1-2 SPY levels. For Lane A low-float names with their own catalyst the index is secondary: not a tick-by-tick veto.
Entry: Market and stock aligned: press; opposed: less aggressive.
Stop: n/a.
Target and exits: SPY at huge resistance: consider trimming or closing longs.
Time: Premarket and before entries.
Refuse if: Forcing longs into a falling market. A scheduled number not yet released (you may not trade until it is out).
