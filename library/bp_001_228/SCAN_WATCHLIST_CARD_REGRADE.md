Conditions: Do not trade a stock before you know its average volume, daily range, key levels, short interest and fresh news. Premarket: grade each name, write one card per A/B-grade name (3-5 cards) with prior-day levels and the invalidation level. Main screen = A-grade, one or two names; second screen = B-grade or one fixable item.
Entry: Trade mostly prepared names; every intraday addition gets a card and the same filters.
Stop: Stop level broken before the trigger = setup void.
Target and exits: n/a.
Time: Premarket; re-grade through the session.
Refuse if: No A- or B-grade card. Re-grade when volume dries, the spread widens or news fades: A-grade at 08:30 can be a reject at 10:30. A stock that stops moving goes to the second screen until volume and clean action return.
