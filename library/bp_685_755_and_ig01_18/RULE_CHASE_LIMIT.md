Conditions: Use a limit at the level. A marketable limit is capped at 3c over the ask, only on a confirmed momentum signal with a pre-set stop.
Entry: Missed trigger: chase at most 3c. 5c only after logged results at 3c.
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Uncapped market order. Slippage of 5-10c is rejected. Chasing more than 3c.
