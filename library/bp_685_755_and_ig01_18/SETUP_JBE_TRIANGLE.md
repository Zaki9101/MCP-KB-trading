Conditions: Flat top (U) with rising lows pressing it; volume dry in the base and up on the break.
Entry: Bar through the flat top, volume rising, ask thinning.
Stop: Price loses the last higher low (or L); beyond the cap = skip.
Target and exits: Vertical bar plus volume peak: bank 25-50% at 2.0R.
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Lows flatten, spike at U, then stall.
