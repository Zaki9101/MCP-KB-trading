Conditions: All times US Eastern (EST or EDT not stated). Desk 7:30-8:00, final list 9:20. From 9:35 only a named open pattern at Tier1 with pre-open structure on the sheet. Open drives until about 10:00.
Entry: Main window 9:35 to 11:00. 11:30 only for a stock still moving clean. Stop at 11:00 or two hours from the first entry, whichever is first.
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: No entries 11:00-13:00. After 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size, flat before the close.
Refuse if: Outside these windows. No new entries 9:20-9:35.
