Conditions: Consolidations with a spike on each break; each step is 50-75% of the last. Channel trail: higher highs and higher lows.
Entry: A base low, or the break of the base high. Add only on a held bounce at the lower line.
Stop: Under each new consolidation low; under the lower line at each pullback.
Target and exits: Trail. A vertical burst through the upper line is an exit.
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Step under 50% or over 75% of the last; support lost; leg 3+ without strong L2; buying the vertical bar.
