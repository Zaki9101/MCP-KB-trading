Conditions: A huge print shows urgency (example 4,000,000 shares).
Entry: Add only to a working trade if ticks down are shown; follow the next ticks.
Stop: Stock does not act: lighten up.
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Adding when the ticks go up (sheet: ticks up = do NOT add) or the stock does not react.
