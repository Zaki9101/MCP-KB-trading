Conditions: Default entry is regular (on the trigger, tape confirms) or conservative (held retest). Aggressive (before the trigger) only if all conditions hold: market trending your way, ask thinning, gradual approach, Tier1 or half lot.
Entry: not stated
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Aggressive entry on an opening-range break. Entry inside a tight consolidation with no jump and no volume signature.
