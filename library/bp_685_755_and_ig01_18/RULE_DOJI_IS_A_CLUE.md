Conditions: A doji (open equals close, long wicks) after 5-10 greens on heavy volume is a reversal clue, a standoff. It is not an entry.
Entry: not stated
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Entry on the doji. Ignore dojis in a flat range.
