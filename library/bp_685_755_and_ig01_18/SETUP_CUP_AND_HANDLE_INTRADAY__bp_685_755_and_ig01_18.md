Conditions: Cup at least 30 minutes; handle at most 50% of cup depth and at most 30% of cup time. Volume dry at the cup bottom and handle low, heavy on the rim break.
Entry: Break above the rim after the handle, heavy volume, Tier1.
Stop: Under the handle low (bottom); beyond the cap = skip.
Target and exits: Half at the volume peak (IG-04). After 25-50% banked: stop to breakeven plus confidence level.
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Cup under 30 min; handle too deep or long; stop beyond cap.
