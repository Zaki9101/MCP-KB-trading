Conditions: Name the day type before choosing a setup: trend, regular range, narrow range, expanding range. Trend: with-trend breakouts and pullbacks, scale out, trail. Range: edge trades after 2 held tests per limit. Narrow: wait for the break.
Entry: not stated
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Expanding or erratic day (breaks fail, bounce back): no trade, stay out. After losses change approach only when the day type changes.
