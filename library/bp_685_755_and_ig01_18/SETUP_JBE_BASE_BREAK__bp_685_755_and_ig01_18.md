Conditions: Jump (JBE) of at least 1.5x the base, 2x preferred; base 25c or less at its high; no sharp pullback; volume dry in the base then rising on the break; index not against; Tier1.
Entry: Break of the base high (U) with the ask thinning, or the held retest. Never inside the base.
Stop: Under the base low (L), inside the 10c/20c cap; beyond the cap = skip.
Target and exits: Bank 25-50% at 2.0R. Exit on a vertical bar with a volume peak. IG-04 says half at the first spike.
Time: Main window 9:35-11:00.
Refuse if: Base over 25c; jump under 1.5x; spike at U; rushing up to U; chasing over 3c; a few cents for 15-20 min with no jump.
