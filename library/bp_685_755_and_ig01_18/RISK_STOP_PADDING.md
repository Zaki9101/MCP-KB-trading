Conditions: Padding goes beyond the level so noise does not hit the stop.
Entry: not stated
Stop: 1-3c past the level; 5-10c past a whole number, half number or range edge. Padding counts in the share size.
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Padding that pushes the stop past the cap: skip.
