Conditions: On 1-min to 5-min momentum entries the chart break must be confirmed by L2 and tape. Volume confirms, it never triggers. Trigger is ask shrinking, bid holding, fast prints.
Entry: Chart break plus L2 and tape confirmation.
Stop: not stated
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: Chart-only breakout on a 1-5 min chart: no trade. Asks re-pricing over static bids: skip. A print above the ask is never a trigger.
