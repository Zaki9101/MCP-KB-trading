Conditions: Stop first: no stop number means no trade. Use a structure stop inside the cent cap. Held-bid setups stop 1-3c under the held bid. Wall stop sits 2c under the wall.
Entry: not stated
Stop: Structure stop; padding 1-3c past the level, 5-10c past a whole or half number. Beyond the cap: skip. Never widen the stop to fit structure.
Target and exits: not stated
Time: not stated
Refuse if: No stop number written first. Wider notes examples: too aggressive, capped.
