Conditions: Halt-prone stock (example: a penny stock after +500% on no news) or a stock that halts.
Entry: reduced size and no margin on halt-prone stocks. Beginners: halts in the simulator only.
Stop: a halted stock can reopen far past your stop (example: open down 20%); a stop cannot limit the gap-through.
Target and exits: assume at least 5 minutes with no exit, much longer on news (Ch.19).
Time: any.
Refuse if: halt-prone stock on margin; halt rules and limit bands not verified with your broker.
