Conditions: Best entry: the first with-trend pullback after a trend-line break (best reward:risk, before the trend is obvious). In a steep, tight channel: wait for the breakout pullback (a higher low that holds) or a pullback within about five bars.
Entry: trigger one tick above the pullback signal bar.
Stop: one tick below the signal bar or the breakout-pullback low, inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: trail below each new higher low; no breakeven before the first partial.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: buying the first single-bar breakout of a steep, tight channel; no trending swings yet; late-day countertrend entries.
