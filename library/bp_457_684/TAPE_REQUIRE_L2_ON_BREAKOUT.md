Conditions: A 1-min to 5-min momentum breakout entry.
Entry: require Level 2/tape confirmation on every breakout entry, never the chart alone. Retail traders who enter on the pattern alone get absorbed at the liquidity point and reversed. No Level 2 pattern on its own is a setup either.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any; Level 2 is too fast in the first minutes of the open.
Refuse if: perfect chart but no L2/tape confirmation (no signal = no trade).
