Conditions: Edge is weaker or the exit is less certain.
Entry: half lot when the stock can skid (wide changing spread, big gaps between levels), levels are broad, or the range is wide. Premarket: half or quarter. Thin book: half. Midday: lowest tier of the day with tight stops. Halt-prone stock: reduced, no margin. When risk per share is large: one-third size at three times normal risk (Brooks, adopted inside Tier1).
Stop: same dollar risk; larger size only on a tight structural stop with enough liquidity.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: not stated.
