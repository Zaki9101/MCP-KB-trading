Conditions: Measure reward:risk before entry, to the first target.
Entry: plan at least 2.0 to the first target. 1.0-2.0 only at half size and only if your own logged win rate on that setup is 60% or more. Never below 1.0. Room to the next level must be larger than the stop.
Stop: per the cent caps.
Target and exits: 2:1 breaks even at a 33% win rate, 1:1 at 50%. 2:1 is the first-batch target, not a cap; the rest is trailed.
Time: any.
Refuse if: no ratio known at entry; ratio under 1.0; first target under 2.0 without the half-size exception.
