Conditions: A surge, a 50% pullback and a higher high on rising volume while the stock is still under VWAP or its moving averages is not momentum.
Entry: ignore it; be interested only above the prior high.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: price under VWAP/MAs; mid-channel; a red shooting star with massive volume and upper wicks at a new high (shooting-star setup: no trade).
