Conditions: Before every session answer: market strength today on 0-10; am I centered, rested and ready; how should I adjust; what is the obvious stock; when was the last massive squeeze; hot or cold cycle. Log one line on the regime.
Entry: trade slowly when the market is slow; get aggressive only on a clear green light. Normal or choppy market: cut size and trade count; throttle up only after the market proves hot (Ch.19).
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: before the open.
Refuse if: expecting a big winner every session regardless of conditions.
