Conditions: Watch the body growth and close of the entry bar and the next two bars.
Entry: a second entry filled at a better price than the first is a warning (good fill, bad trade): re-check the read.
Stop: not stated beyond the plan.
Target and exits: strong closes: hold more shares for more points. Weak closes or a doji entry bar: scalp more, or exit near breakeven. Take a partial at the first reasonable target, move the stop to breakeven, let the rest run in a strong trend. Exit on a breakout failure: a bear reversal bar with follow-through against the bull spike.
Time: first three bars after entry.
Refuse if: not stated.
