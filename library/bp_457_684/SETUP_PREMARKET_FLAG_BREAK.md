Conditions: Good premarket chart: orderly climb with higher lows above rising 9/20 EMAs, volume building, a tight sideways-to-down flag near the high (NAV 5-min example, flag about 18.0-18.6).
Entry: wait for the break of the flag or the premarket high; require confirmation above the premarket high. A good break comes with the day's biggest volume bar and a run of 10-15%.
Stop: not stated (Ch.19 caps: 10c on $1-$8 stocks, 20c otherwise).
Target and exits: not stated; in the example price pulled back to the 9 EMA after the spike.
Time: premarket at half size or less; no new entries 9:20-9:35 (Ch.19).
Refuse if: one vertical spike then a descending channel (the move is over); price falls back through the EMAs before the open; the opening volume spike only sells off; it fails to break the premarket flag high or sells off fast at the open.
