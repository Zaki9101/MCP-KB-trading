Conditions: Asks push lower and bids do not follow; new asks keep reappearing lower; a thin or scarce bid sits opposite a thick ask. Price may drop, and drop fast.
Entry: do not buy. Ch.19 Level 2 gaps: A-grade $0.01 or less between levels; B-grade up to $0.02 at half size.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: gaps wider than $0.02; any gap on the bid side; a thin bid under a thick ask.
