Conditions: Any day-trade position near the close.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: the day account is flat at the close; all losers out before the close. Example: a falling stock bought near $15 opened near $8 the next day (over 50% of the account). Overnight holds only in a separate swing account with its own risk, hard stops, a hedge plan and smaller size.
Time: before the close.
Refuse if: holding a day trade overnight.
