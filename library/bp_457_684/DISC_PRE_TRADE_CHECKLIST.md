Conditions: All YES before the order: a setup in MY PlayBook by name; reason is a price/volume signal; inside my window, not 9:20-9:35, no trigger fired, no cap reached; day type classified and the setup fits; stock moving, clean, directional.
Entry: plan in one sentence in about 2 seconds (exit if wrong AND if right); if-then sheet written; labelled Move2Move or Trade2Hold with Reasons2Sell written; L2/tape confirms the chart.
Stop: set BEFORE entry; ratio at least 2:1; room to the next level larger than the stop; spread and slippage acceptable.
Target and exits: Reasons2Sell written before entry.
Time: before every order.
Refuse if: FOMO 50% or more; no four deep breaths; chasing (first 10-20c gone); third attempt; re-buy of a failed reversal; size outside tier or not fitted to liquidity; halt-prone stock without reduced size and no margin; tired, unprepared or on tilt.
