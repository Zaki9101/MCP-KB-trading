Conditions: Bull trend or just after a big bull trend bar. Inside bar: high at or below the prior high, low at or above the prior low; small, ideally with a body opposite the trend. Stacks: ii (two inside bars), iii, ioi.
Entry: trigger one tick above the inside bar high (ii: above both bars). The book works stop orders both ways, first fill decides; this long-only card takes the upside fill. Verify paired-order cancels with your broker.
Stop: beyond the opposite side (one tick below the inside bar low, or both bars of an ii); tighten after the entry bar closes; Ch.19 caps govern.
Target and exits: at least a scalp; a second leg up (ioi).
Time: main window.
Refuse if: doji inside bar; large doji; inside bar in the middle of a trading range. Exit on a breakout failure within the next several bars (common after ii).
