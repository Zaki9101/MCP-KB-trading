Conditions: Default entry is regular (on the trigger, tape confirms) or conservative (held retest).
Entry: aggressive (before the trigger) only if ALL hold: market trending your way, buying pressure rising (ask thinning), gradual approach, Tier1 or half the planned lot, stop at the structure. Order: limit at the level; marketable limit capped at 3c over the ask only on a confirmed momentum signal with a pre-set stop. Missed trigger: chase at most 3c.
Stop: at the structure, inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: an aggressive entry on an opening-range break; an uncapped market order.
