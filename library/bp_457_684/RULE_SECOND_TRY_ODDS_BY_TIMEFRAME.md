Conditions: Deciding whether to wait for a second chance at an entry (author's own estimates).
Entry: 60-minute chart: about 70/30 in favour of getting a second try, so prefer waiting. 15-minute: about 50/50, use judgement. 1-5 minute (fast intraday): odds not stated; in practice you usually must take the first entry.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: not stated.
