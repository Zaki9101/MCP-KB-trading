Conditions: Clear bull trend with four or more consecutive non-climactic, non-overlapping trend bars; you missed some or all of the first entry.
Entry: buy only the shares you would still hold had you taken the first entry, with a marketable limit capped at 3c over the ask (Ch.19). Book example: 300 shares planned, 100 still held after bar 4: buy 100.
Stop: the original entry's stop or breakeven; dollar risk stays at the original plan; caps apply to total risk.
Target and exits: swing portion; add on pullbacks; trail one tick below the last signal bar.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: you would hold none (do not enter late); trend bars overlapping or climactic; risking more dollars because of open profit.
