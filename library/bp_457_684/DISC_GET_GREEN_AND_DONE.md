Conditions: Beginner, small account, or just after a big loss or drawdown.
Entry: "get green and shut it down": after the first profitable trade, or when the window ends, or a stop rule fires, stop. If the first or second trade is red, trade again only if the right pattern develops. Keep this until the first drawdown is recovered.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: green day: exit early rather than risk going green to red. Once you decide you are done on a good day, pull profit and quit.
Time: whole session.
Refuse if: "one last trade" half an hour after deciding you are done (willpower is at zero); revenge or "get it back" urges.
