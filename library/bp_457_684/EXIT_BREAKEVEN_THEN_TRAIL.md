Conditions: An open trade; watch the tape, not the P&L.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: move to breakeven only once the first partial is sold or the first target is reached; not earlier in a strong trend. Then raise the stop by hand to the newest structure (staircase low, channel line, last higher low, Fibonacci level). The hard stop (breakeven or better) stays live on all shares. Move a stop only after the entry bar closes.
Target and exits: exit on your stop or your signal.
Time: any.
Refuse if: trailing under a level with no real support or on a few ticks of noise; moving the whole stop to breakeven before the first partial.
