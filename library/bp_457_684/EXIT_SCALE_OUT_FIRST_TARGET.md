Conditions: An open winner approaching its first defined target or resistance.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: after the partial, stop to breakeven.
Target and exits: bank 25-50% at the first defined target or resistance. Never none, never just "to feel comfortable". Sell before the target only on a defined tape signal (price/volume spike, spreads widening, bids thinning). A strong morning trend does not protect an open profit; bank partials on strength.
Time: any.
Refuse if: a half / quarter / quarter ladder with fewer than 500 shares (200 shares is not worth halving; 100 cannot scale).
