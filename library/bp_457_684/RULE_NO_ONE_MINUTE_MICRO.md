Conditions: Work from the 5-minute chart. Trading 1-minute micro setups usually loses: too many losers, too few winners, and the best ones trigger too fast to take.
Entry: the Ch.5 1-minute pullback (T/G/L) is allowed only as a trigger refinement inside an established 5-minute with-trend setup. 1-minute entries otherwise only as scalps with a recorded win rate.
Stop: never tighten a stop by moving to a 1-minute chart.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: a standalone 1-minute countertrend trade; reversal bars hunted on a tick or 1-minute chart.
