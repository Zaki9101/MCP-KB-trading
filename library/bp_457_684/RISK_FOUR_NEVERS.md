Conditions: Every trade, every day.
Entry: never add to a loser; never double size after losses (Brooks: the martingale is mathematically unworkable).
Stop: never widen a stop.
Target and exits: at max loss, sell: no averaging down (a $200 stop with adds became $1,000+), no justifying a loser, no turning an intraday trade into an investment.
Time: any.
Refuse if: trading past the daily stop.
