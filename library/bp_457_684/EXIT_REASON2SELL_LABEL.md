Conditions: Before entry, label the trade Move2Move or Trade2Hold and write its Reasons2Sell.
Entry: no label and no written Reasons2Sell = not ready to enter.
Stop: per plan.
Target and exits: Move2Move: exit per plan. Trade2Hold: hold until a Reason2Sell appears, then sell; if the stock keeps going, ignore it. Selling before a Reason2Sell is an error. Exit by planned levels (resistance, volume peak, confidence level), not by mood.
Time: any.
Refuse if: taking any profit because you feel confident, or holding a winner to make up a bad run.
