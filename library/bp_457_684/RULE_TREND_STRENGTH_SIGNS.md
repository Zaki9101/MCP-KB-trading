Conditions: Strength (trend continues): small sideways pullbacks of a few ticks; no two consecutive closes on the other side of the MA; trend bars with little overlap and small tails; a limit buy at the prior close not filled; the trend runs far through several levels.
Entry: keep taking with-trend entries while strength signs hold.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: weakness (reversal or range): pullbacks larger than the first one; two-sided rally with overlapping bars, bear bodies and tails on bull bars; shrinking stairs; a third push overshooting the channel line with a strong reversal bar; trend line broken then a lower high. A strong move corrects in at least two legs, often about 10 bars. A trend of 30+ bars can end with an unusually large exhaustion bar.
Time: any.
Refuse if: not stated.
