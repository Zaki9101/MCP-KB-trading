Conditions: The major trend is up. A micro trend line spans 2 to about 10 small bars, most touching it; one bar breaks the line and the next bar reverses back above the breakout bar's high. A bear micro channel inside a bull trend is a bull flag.
Entry: trigger one tick above the high of the breakout bar (high 1); if not filled, move the buy stop to the next bar's high. Bull-flag version: above the signal bar high.
Stop: editor: below the signal bar, inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: prior swing high or measured move; Brooks scalp example 30-50 cents on AMZN, scalps need the win-rate check. Tier1.
Time: main window, 5-minute chart.
Refuse if: micro channel at the top of a trading range; bear trend day; first breakout of a steep, tight channel; 1-minute micro setups (most lose).
