Conditions: Bull signal bar minimum: close above the open or the bar midpoint. Better: lower tail one-third to one-half of the bar, little upper tail; open near or below the prior close; close above several prior closes (stronger: above the past eight closes, high above the past five highs). Next bar a strong entry bar.
Entry: beginners enter only when the signal bar is a bull trend bar in a bull trend. A small bar is a setup only with a body in the trade direction.
Stop: one tick below a small signal bar when context is strong.
Target and exits: doji entry bar after a reversal: about 50% trade below it within a few bars; exit near breakeven.
Time: any.
Refuse if: doji inside bar; large doji (one-bar range); large top tail; midpoint above the prior bar low (likely a range).
