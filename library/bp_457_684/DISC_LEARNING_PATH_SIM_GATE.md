Conditions: Sim: one proven strategy, copied before customizing, results logged per strategy.
Entry: go live only after 5-6 green sim weeks in a row AND the first drawdown recovered. Live start: 100 shares, 10,000 shares a day, one setup type at a time.
Stop: keep early losses small; many run out of capital before they learn.
Target and exits: plan capital and life for 12-24 months of learning; the gates, not the calendar, decide size. Expand only by adding one proven setup at a time.
Time: not applicable.
Refuse if: treating sim or paper profits as proof; tiny real money as a sim substitute; fades, halts or hunches as a new trader; cannot make money in the sim.
