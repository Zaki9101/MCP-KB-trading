Conditions: Tilt = an angry, frustrated or disappointed state that drives decisions you normally would not make; a bigger-than-expected loss is the usual trigger.
Entry: (1) Detect: hijack signs, long against a short bias, just took a rip, a small loss turned into a stop-out, venting, trying not to finish red. (2) Interrupt: turn from the screen, four deep breaths; if not objective, walk away. (3) Leave: ten-minute walk; return only calm. (4) If you must look: only stocks that moved away from prices, allow defined risk and have room in the trend direction.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: train it: 15 minutes daily, visualize the last upsetting trade.
Time: any.
Refuse if: pounding heart, clammy palms or tunnel vision after a big loss or win: computer off for the rest of the day.
