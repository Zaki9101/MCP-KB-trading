Conditions: Account setup rules, fixed before the session.
Entry: leverage (an international broker allows up to 6x) changes share count, never dollar risk. No margin until 6-12 months profitable on your own cash. Fees of $200+ a month must be earned back before profit counts (editor note).
Stop: a small account has zero cushion: hold stops religiously; no overnight positions.
Target and exits: withdraw profits above a fixed base and keep the trading amount constant; check broker minimum equity first.
Time: not applicable.
Refuse if: running at the $25,000 minimum (any dip forces a top-up; verify pattern-day-trader rules with your broker). A $500 account is proof of concept only.
