Conditions: All times US Eastern.
Entry: premarket 7:00-9:20 half size or less. 9:20-9:30 no orders; no new entries 9:20-9:35. From 9:35 only a named open pattern at Tier1 with your normal stop; open-drive entries only until about 10:00; beginners sit out the first minutes.
Stop: normal stop; exit at once if an open trade goes against you.
Target and exits: main window 9:35-11:00 (11:30 only for a stock still moving clean); stop at 11:00 or two hours from the first entry, whichever is first.
Time: 11:00-13:00 no new entries. After 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size, flat before the close. Final hour: trade only when both you and the setup are strong.
Refuse if: a meeting or errand falls in trading hours (do not trade that day).
