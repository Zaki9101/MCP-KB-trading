Conditions: A beaten-down stock, or the urge to "buy low, sell high".
Entry: no assumed bounce. Buy low only at capitulation (selling exhausted) or a double bottom (support holds); those are separate reversal setups. Capitulation has no measurable trigger: start with 50-100 shares.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: buying a stock in a downtrend because it looks cheap (each new low is sold); repeated bottom-picking against the trend (SNDK: repeated buys and stops).
