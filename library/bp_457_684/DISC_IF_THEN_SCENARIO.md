Conditions: The night before, write an if-then sheet per candidate and per gap scenario: not "the market will do X" but "if the market does X, I do Y". Decide the mode first: trend (trade with it) or range.
Entry: illustrative range 19.75-20.00: breaks above 20.00 = buy; never breaks 20.00 = no trigger, no trade. When a signal triggers, enter without hesitation.
Stop: after the buy, back to 19.75 = exit. The 25-cent distance is the example's, not a stop rule: take the stop from your setup.
Target and exits: when the system says sell, close. A loss is a foreseen scenario.
Time: night before and before the open.
Refuse if: no written branch for what price is doing; doubting the system live (test it on paper instead).
