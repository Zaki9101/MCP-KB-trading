Conditions: Just stopped out, and the stock moves the other way or sets up again.
Entry: no automatic flip or re-entry. Any new trade needs its own PlayBook trigger, a fresh stop, the full checklist including four breaths.
Stop: the new trade carries its own cap and counts toward the daily stop.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: any tilt sign; jumping back in to get the loss back. Reversals after a stop are the most common way a losing morning becomes a losing day.
