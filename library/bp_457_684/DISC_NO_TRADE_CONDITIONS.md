Conditions: Check before every order.
Entry: none if any item below holds.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: 9:20-9:35; a stop-trading trigger fired or a cap is reached; FOMO over 50%; no named PlayBook setup; the reason is news, value or opinion; bored, down, revenge or need; stock not moving, 50-50 or unreadable; day type not classified, or a trend setup on a range day; third or later attempt at a level; perfect chart but no L2/tape confirmation; stop larger than the room to the next level; tilted, unprepared or late; inside a tight few-cent consolidation over 15-20 minutes; expanding range; extreme news (wait for the dust to settle). Tape mixed, Level 2 unclear, slow prints, wide spread or guessing: never a large position.
