Conditions: A level you already traded today.
Entry: a re-entry needs a new signal, a new stop and half the previous size. Only the first and second attempts at a level are valid.
Stop: fresh stop per attempt, inside the caps.
Target and exits: after two stop-outs in a stock, drop it for the day. Small-cap runner: the first failed re-entry ends the stock (Ch.19).
Time: per day.
Refuse if: a third attempt at the same level; re-buying at once to get it back; re-buying a failed reversal long without evidence it can hold (YHOO 18.10 failed at 18.30).
