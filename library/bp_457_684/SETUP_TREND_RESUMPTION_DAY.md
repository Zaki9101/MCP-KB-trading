Conditions: Strong bull trend in the first hour or two, then a tight range for hours. A breakout against the morning trend between 14:00 and 15:00 ET fails (trap), followed by a two- or three-bar spike up.
Entry: after the trap fails, buy the breakout pullback in the morning trend direction. Tier1 first entry; adds only inside the total-risk rule.
Stop: below the breakout-pullback low or the failed reversal bar, beyond the trap wick, inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: measured move from the spike's open to its final bar close; prior range top. Exit on the target or if a reversal bar at the range edge fails.
Time: Ch.19: after 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size, flat before the close.
Refuse if: the midday range breaks against the morning trend and holds (possible reversal day); trading the trap breakout itself.
