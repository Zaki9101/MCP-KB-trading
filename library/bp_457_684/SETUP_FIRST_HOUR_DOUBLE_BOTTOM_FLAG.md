Conditions: First hour: double tops and bottoms often appear together and put the market in breakout mode (GS 5-min example: lower low, retest, higher low, inside bars).
Entry: buy above the ii after the double bottom, or above the double bottom bull flag high. Add on the breakout pullback (adds only to a winner).
Stop: below the double bottom low until the higher low forms, then below the higher low; inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: Ch.19: no new entries 9:20-9:35; from 9:35 only a named open pattern at Tier1.
Refuse if: a tricky open, until the second pattern confirms.
