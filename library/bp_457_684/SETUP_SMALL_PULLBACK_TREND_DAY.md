Conditions: Trend from the open with the day's low in the first few bars; every pullback small (about 10-30% of ADR) and price keeps closing above the MA. Forms about once or twice a month.
Entry: buy at or 1-2 ticks below the prior bar's low, or one tick above a high 1 or high 2 signal bar. Adds: Tier1 each; total risk equals one normal trade.
Stop: stocks: Ch.8 cent caps (the book's 2-point stop is ES). Do not move to breakeven early.
Target and exits: trail below the swing low after each new high. Take profits on an exhaustive buy climax after 14:00 ET followed by a two-legged pullback (about two-thirds of these days, often 150-200% of the earlier pullback).
Time: from 9:35; larger pullback expected 14:00-15:00 ET.
Refuse if: a close back below the MA as the day turns into a range; pullbacks larger than the first.
