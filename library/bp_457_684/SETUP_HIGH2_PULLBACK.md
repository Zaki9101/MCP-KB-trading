Conditions: Bull trend with an ABC pullback and earlier strength (a trend break or a high 1 signal). High 1 = first bar whose high exceeds the prior bar's high after the pullback starts; high 2 = the next such bar. Strong bull reversal signal bar. In a bull channel, best is a high 2 at the MA, not near the channel top.
Entry: trigger one tick above the high 2 signal bar. High 1 only in a strong trend with a strong signal bar. High 3 (wedge bull flag): first signal at the third leg.
Stop: editor: below the C-leg low or signal bar low, inside the caps; trail under higher lows after the entry bar closes.
Target and exits: editor: prior swing high, then trail.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: counting in a trading range; doji inside bar signal; no earlier strength; an unconfirmed high 4.
