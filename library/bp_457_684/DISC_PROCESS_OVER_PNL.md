Conditions: A trade is good if the plan and the seven fundamentals were followed (preparation, hard work, patience, a detailed plan before every trade, discipline, communication, replaying important trades), not because of its P&L.
Entry: grade the day on rule adherence.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: exited per plan and the stock went on: accepted cost, no regret (about 1 point on BRCM). Do not wait for the top print; the costly decisions are in the last 10-20 cents.
Time: after each trade and after the close.
Refuse if: celebrating after a profit; letting one trade change your mood or your discipline; redrawing a chart in review.
