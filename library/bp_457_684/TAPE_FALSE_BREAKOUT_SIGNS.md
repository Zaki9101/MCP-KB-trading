Conditions: False breakouts are the #1 account killer: a few cents through a level, then a flush through recent longs' stops. No method avoids them 100%.
Entry: check the last 10-30 minutes for flush history (even a big flush candle three candles back). A bigger pullback before the break gives higher odds.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: steep up candle through resistance followed by an even steeper candle down: expect a deep red candle.
Time: any.
Refuse if: multiple upper wicks at the level (2+ with closes below = move away or wait until it leaves the channel); a close below the level; no bid support at the level; chasing the break without these confirmations; buying the steep breakout candle. Mark the false-break high and do not touch it until it breaks.
