Conditions: Trade only plays you can read; an unfamiliar situation means observe.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: large institutional caps or low-volume stocks (momentum never works there); stocks you cannot read or 50-50 stocks; the day's hot name you cannot read; a group no longer In Play; a stock barely moving or whose rhythm and volume stopped giving clues; wide-spread high-priced names; an order book too thick to move fast; wicky candles (risk not worth the reward); gappy flat or doji candles with no trend (never trade).
