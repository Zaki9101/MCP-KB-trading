Conditions: Gap and a strong first bar; opening range under about 25% of ADR (breakout mode). Trend-from-the-open days are about 1 in 5 (Emini).
Entry: from 9:35 only (Ch.19), at Tier1: trigger one tick above a strong first bar; the high-probability trade is the first pullback after a spike of 3+ bars (scalp it).
Stop: editor: other end of the first bar or the opening range, inside the cent cap (the book's 2-point stop is an ES value).
Target and exits: scalp on the first pullback; measured move up equal to open-to-low or the initial leg height.
Time: open-drive entries only until about 10:00 (Ch.19).
Refuse if: inside or overlapping first bar; two hours sideways; a small first range near the prior range. If stopped, any reversal is a new trade that counts toward the daily stop.
