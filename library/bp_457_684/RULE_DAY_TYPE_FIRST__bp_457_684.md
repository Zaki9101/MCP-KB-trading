Conditions: Name the day type before choosing setups: trend, regular range, narrow range, expanding range or erratic. Brooks' first-60-minute check: opening range under about 25% of ADR = breakout mode, trend from the open possible; about 1/3 to 1/2 = trending trading range day; large gap or first-hour spike = spike and channel. A one-bar pause after a spike suggests spike and channel; a 5-10 bar sideways pause suggests a range.
Entry: trend day: with-trend breakouts and pullbacks. Narrow range: wait for the break.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: first 60 minutes. ES thresholds: check against the stock's own ADR.
Refuse if: expanding range or erratic day (no trade); trend setups on a range day.
