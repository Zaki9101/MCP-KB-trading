Conditions: Measure the pullback before a long re-entry.
Entry: F to A (about 20%) holds: a fake is less likely. A to B (about 20-35%): Level 2 and price action must be strong. Below B (35%+): use channels and wait for a return to F. A deep pullback is monitor-only until a hammer, a higher pivot low, a break of the nearest level, then Level 2.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: a pullback retraces 75% or more of the prior swing (Brooks): odds of a return to the prior extreme fall, treat it as a possible trend change, not a pullback.
