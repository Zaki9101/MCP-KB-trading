Conditions: After a red day, a giveback breach, a stop violation or three bad trades.
Entry: smaller size and fewer trades, never bigger. In a bad drawdown: max share size and max daily loss at 50% or 25% of regular, set with the broker; one trade a day for 5 days, aiming for 5 small green days. Low confidence or a slump: lower the tier, best setups at your best timeframes.
Stop: cut losses at once.
Target and exits: restore the guardrails only after about half of the drawdown is recouped.
Time: next sessions until recovered.
Refuse if: raising tier to escape a slump. Cannot get out of losing trades: trade the demo until you can (doubling down = 3 days on demo).
