Conditions: A large displayed bid or ask block on Level 2. Price may crash straight through it, or it may be pulled without executing (spoofing); real buyers and sellers usually hide their size.
Entry: a wall is a hint, never a trigger. Act only after real prints test it and it holds or fails. Use Level 2 to time the entry and judge slippage; take direction from prints and the price/volume bars.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: repeated wicks plus a Level 2 wall plus fast, stuck tape at a level (avoid).
