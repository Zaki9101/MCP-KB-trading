Conditions: A structure with a measurable height. Formulas are ES averages applied as structure: check against the stock's own ADR.
Entry: the first target must still give at least 2.0R (Ch.19).
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: channel or range breakout that succeeds: measured move equal to the channel or range height. Spike and channel: open of the first spike bar to the high or close of the last spike bar, projected from the channel start. Wedge breakout: wedge height from the wedge start. Range breakout: height of the lower range (smallest range if several). Trend from the open: open-to-low or the initial leg height. Several targets clustering at one price make a stronger target, not a trigger.
Time: any.
Refuse if: not stated.
