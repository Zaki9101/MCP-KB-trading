Conditions: Chart the account after every day as an ascending channel. Scale ACTIVITY with the curve (how many valid setups you take), never SIZE inside a day or week.
Entry: above the upper line: take every valid setup within the caps. Account falls with the market while you buy false breakouts: cut activity drastically.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: near-vertical equity rise (rarely lasts more than about 3 days): be less aggressive, no size increase.
Time: end of day.
Refuse if: account under the lower channel line = one day off, then less activity until back inside; the same equity level fails twice = one week off, observe only, resume at the current tier.
