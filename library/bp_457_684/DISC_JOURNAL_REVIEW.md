Conditions: One journal row per trade, filled while fresh: date, time, symbol, setup name, day type, label, plan sentence, shares, entry, stop, target, ratio at entry, exit price and reason, net P&L, hold time, rules followed Y/N, FOMO %, one state tag (calm / tilt + trigger).
Entry: not stated.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: after a loss, write 3 factual lines (what happened, not how it felt). Post-trade review (2 minutes): plan followed, label right, exit by plan.
Time: right after each trade and after the close.
Refuse if: postponing the journal. Daily review: count trades outside setups; check that trade count fell after a loss; write what to eliminate tomorrow. Weekly: someone else reviews your trades.
