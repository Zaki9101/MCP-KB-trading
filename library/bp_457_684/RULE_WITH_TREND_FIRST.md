Conditions: Take every valid with-trend entry; buy pullbacks in a bull trend, do not sell them. In a strong trend most signal bars look weak.
Entry: a countertrend entry needs all three: the wider chart supports a reversal, it follows a strong trend-line break, and the reversal bar has a strong body closing near its extreme. Maximum 2 countertrend trades a day, Tier1 only.
Stop: per the cent caps.
Target and exits: about 80% of trend-reversal attempts fail and become flags.
Time: any.
Refuse if: buying the first reversal attempt after four or more consecutive bear trend bars (or two or three large ones): wait for a second attempt; a countertrend move is not a reversal until its second leg forms.
