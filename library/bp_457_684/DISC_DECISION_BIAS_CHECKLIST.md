Conditions: Before the trade: write the loss limit and the exit; list what you do not know (float, news, tape, higher-timeframe context) and lower confidence until gathered; write one piece of chart, tape or Level 2 evidence that would prove the idea wrong.
Entry: pre-mortem: assume it failed, write the three likeliest reasons, fix any your stop, size or thesis does not cover. A clean, confident setup is the one to slow down for. Decide when attention is free.
Stop: moving or removing the protective stop needs a written, logged override.
Target and exits: write your own fair value for the target before looking at the prior high or an analyst price.
Time: before entry.
Refuse if: a multi-condition setup assumed certain (five conditions at 80% each all hold only about 33% of the time); expecting a win after a losing streak.
