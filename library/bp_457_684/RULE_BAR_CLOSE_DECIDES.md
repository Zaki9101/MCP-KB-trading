Conditions: A bar can look like a reversal at minute 3 and close on its low; a bar can grow 4+ ticks in its last 5-10 seconds. The close is the price that counts.
Entry: wait for the signal bar to close, or for a second entry; until consistently profitable, wait for the second entry after a late change.
Stop: keep the initial stop until the entry bar closes; never move it to just below a bar still forming.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: last seconds of each bar.
Refuse if: acting on a forming bar; picking a bottom in a strong trend before the bar closes; tightening the stop by switching to a 1-minute chart (smaller frames take stop runs).
