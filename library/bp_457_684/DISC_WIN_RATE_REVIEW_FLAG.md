Conditions: Track win rate, profit/loss ratio, net versus gross and day-level consistency together. No stand-alone win-rate target: positive NET expectancy over a real sample and a ratio of at least 2:1. Benchmark 65% at 2:1; 75% is a stretch.
Entry: 60%+ with 1:5 is the standard for sizing a setup UP.
Stop: 30-40% of well-planned trades still stop out; a stop-out on a valid setup is expected.
Target and exits: a month under 50% winners = review. Under 50% AND average win below average loss (net): paper trade. Under 50% with ratio 2:1+ and a positive net month: current tier, no size-up. Ratio 1:1-2:1: tier down one step, cut the weakest setups.
Time: monthly.
Refuse if: sizing up on a win-rate goal.
