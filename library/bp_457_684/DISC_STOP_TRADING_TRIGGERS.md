Conditions: Run the list after every trade. Any ONE trigger = close positions and stop for the day (platform off).
Entry: none once a trigger fires.
Stop: triggers: daily max loss hit; 30% giveback of peak profit (15% is a 10-minute break and half size, not a stop); three consecutive full-risk losers (scratches under 25% of the per-trade max do not count); window over (two hours from the first trade or 11:00); trade, ticket or stock cap reached, or one trade outside your setups already taken; "get green" met (beginner, after a drawdown, small account); hijack signs; tilt not gone after four breaths and a ten-minute walk; revenge or "one more trade" urge; a sizing violation (demo at least 3 days); late, unprepared or distracted.
Target and exits: flat.
Time: whole session.
Refuse if: any trigger has fired.
