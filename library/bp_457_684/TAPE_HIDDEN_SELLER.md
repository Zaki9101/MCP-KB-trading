Conditions: Price flat while volume rises steadily; Time & Sales shows prints absorbed on the ASK. That is a hidden or massive seller; check Time & Sales first (volume is only secondary confirmation).
Entry: avoid the long. Heavy prints that do not move price, or bid support at the level that disappears despite apparent buying: warning signs, not confirmation; wait for price to actually move with the prints.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: the usual outcome is a downside break.
Time: any.
Refuse if: testing hidden orders with your own order.
