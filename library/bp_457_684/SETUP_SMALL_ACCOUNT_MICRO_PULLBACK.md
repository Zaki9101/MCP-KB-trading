Conditions: Only leading gappers or leading relative-volume stocks, never a random stock; A-quality (all five scan criteria). Front side of the move while the stock moves quickly; 1-min and 5-min charts.
Entry: buy the micro-pullback or dip on the front side. The exact trigger is not reproduced; the handbook points to Ch.5 1-Min Pullback, First Pullback After a Break of VWAP, and Ch.7 S12 Red-to-Green.
Stop: not stated (Ch.8 caps and Tier1 sizing).
Target and exits: get in, get green, get out; plan about 2:1. Exit on MACD crossing its signal line, falling volume, or a "Jackknife Rejection" (undefined).
Time: 7:00-9:20 half size or less, nothing 9:20-9:35, stop at 11:00 (Ch.19).
Refuse if: not a leading gapper; back side of the move; holding and hoping instead of cutting the loss.
