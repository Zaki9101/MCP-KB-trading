Conditions: After a bull spike and channel, the correction returns to the start of the channel (the gap). The book says the start is usually tested within a day or two.
Entry: buy one tick above the reversal bar that forms a double bottom with the channel bottom, or above the first bull reversal bar after the test.
Stop: below the test low; a larger stop means a smaller size; inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: editor: channel high or prior swing high; measured move from the spike. Day account flat at the close (Ch.19).
Time: intraday only.
Refuse if: buying the first bull bar with no double bottom or reversal bar; treating a tight channel as tradable both ways.
