Conditions: A breakout attempt at resistance.
Entry: if resistance already showed strong volume and then a volume spike hits right on the breakout attempt, treat it as a warning, not confirmation: wait for a hold and a dry-volume pullback before buying. A sharp price jump on low volume can be a bull trap, a market-maker test or a stop-hunt: wait for volume to confirm.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: buying the spike bar itself in either case.
