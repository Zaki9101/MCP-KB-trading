Conditions: Wedge bull flag: a pullback of three pushes down between converging lines (often a high 3). Failed low 2 variant: after a sell-off, a second push down fails to make a lower low, followed by a strong follow-through bar; the second push can form a double bottom bull flag.
Entry: wedge: buy above the wedge flag high. Failed low 2: buy stop above the bar that breaks the failed push.
Stop: below the wedge bottom or the first push low (no number in the book); a wedge stop often breaks the cap on low-priced names: skip (v1 rule R03).
Target and exits: a test of the start of the wedge or channel, at least two legs.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: the breakout after the failed low 2 does not follow through; reading one bar instead of the sequence of pushes.
