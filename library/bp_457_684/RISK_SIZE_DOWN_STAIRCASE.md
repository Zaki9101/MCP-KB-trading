Conditions: Down-triggers, checked daily.
Entry: 2 negative days: basics only (5-min setups or about 10M-share stocks), summit before the next session. 3 negative days: review all trades that evening; Tier1 only until every trade is reviewed (Ch.19); only money-making plays, lock gains quickly. 5-7 days of struggle or a slump: half size for a week; after the 2nd positive day add a little each day. Midday as your weak period: lowest tier, tightest loss limit.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: next sessions.
Refuse if: you broke a sizing rule: demo at least 3 days (second violation: demo again). Negative for months: reduce size, review quarterly, re-evaluate the style.
