Conditions: Strong bull trend (four or more consecutive trend bars, or two or three large ones). The first bear reversal attempt visibly fails, e.g. a large bear bar with a big bottom tail that closes above its midpoint.
Entry: buy the second signal: above the outside up bar, or above the bull bar after the failed bear bar. A small bull reversal bar closing near its high is a scalp-only option.
Stop: editor: below the failed-attempt bar low or the small higher low, inside the Ch.19 caps.
Target and exits: test of the start of the bull channel and the high of the day (no number). Exit if the parabolic slope flattens and a bar closes weak, or momentum reverses on the next bar.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: a second fill better than the first (good fill, bad trade: re-check); scalping a final flag without a stop reference.
