Conditions: A large bull trend bar breaks a swing high, trend line or MA by many ticks; the spike grows 5-10 bars with no pullback over one bar; micro gaps add strength. The first pullback comes after 3+ breakout bars, lasts 1-2 bars, is under one-quarter of the bar height and does not reach the breakout point or entry.
Entry: trigger one tick above the first pullback's signal bar (see RULE_STOP_ENTRY_ONE_TICK).
Stop: book gives none; editor: beyond the pullback extreme, inside the Ch.19 caps (10c on $1-$8, 20c other) or skip (v1 rule R03).
Target and exits: measured move (no number); partial at the first reasonable target, then breakeven.
Time: main window 9:35-11:00.
Refuse if: pullback reaches the breakout point or entry, lasts over 2 bars or follows fewer than 3 breakout bars; weak body, many tails, no follow-through. Brooks' 10-20x volume is Emini only.
