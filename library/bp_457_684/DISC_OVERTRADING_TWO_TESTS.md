Conditions: Either breach = overtrading. Test 1: any trade outside a named PlayBook setup, even trade number one. Test 2: outside the caps: 2 stocks a day (6 ceiling), 20 tickets at 300 shares, new-trader limits, 3 full-risk losers, 2 tries per level, platform off at window end.
Entry: a valid setup never overrides a cap.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: after a losing day, activity goes DOWN; more trades after a loss is revenge.
Time: whole session.
Refuse if: symptoms show: win rate near 40% (or under 50% with ratio under 1:1), net-positive tickers 38.5%, the .01-.09 band netting negative, many feelers and scratches, more trades on losing days than winning days.
