Conditions: News and catalysts select the stock; they never give the entry.
Entry: enter only when price and volume show a setup you recognize. Ask where and how far, not why. If the stock trades against your premarket bias, drop the bias and trade what it shows.
Stop: exit on your own stop or signal, never because of others' opinions.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: the only reason is news, "value", a tip, a product story, a prediction, a guru or paid pick; a gap up on news that fails (Costco example); a stock that merely fell to a Fibonacci level; an indicator divergence with no prior trend-line break.
