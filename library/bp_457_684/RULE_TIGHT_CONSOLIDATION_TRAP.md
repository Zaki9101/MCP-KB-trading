Conditions: Price holds within a few cents for 15-20+ minutes (AIG 24.90-25.10 faked both ways): a "death trap".
Entry: no jump and no volume signature = no trade inside it or on its first break. Trade only a failed break AND a hold above the top, at Tier1 (Ch.19). A TR base that meets every measurement is the exception (separate setup).
Stop: minimum losses only if caught inside.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: trading inside the consolidation; a narrow sideways band with no catalyst.
