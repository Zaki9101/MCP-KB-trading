Conditions: Before reading candles for a long, check the longer-term trend first. A long against a longer-term bearish trend is automatically higher risk and potentially shorter-lived. Ch.19 (new trader): A-grade requires price above the 9, 20, 50, 100 and 200 daily SMA.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: expect a shorter move when trading against the higher-timeframe trend.
Time: premarket and before each long.
Refuse if: price between daily averages, unless you are experienced and at reduced size (Ch.19).
