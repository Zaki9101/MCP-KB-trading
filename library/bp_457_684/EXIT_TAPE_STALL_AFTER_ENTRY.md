Conditions: Time & Sales suddenly stops and price stalls right after you enter.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: the hard stop stays live.
Target and exits: treat it as a possibly mistimed entry: sell for breakeven or a small loss rather than hoping it resumes. Ch.19 time stop: Level 2 momentum trade about 1 minute without progress; Trade2Hold 30 minutes without progress; never hold a stuck trade for hours.
Time: first minute after entry.
Refuse if: holding and hoping the move resumes.
