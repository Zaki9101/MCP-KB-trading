Conditions: A vertical gap or spike on a buyout-type headline, tweet or press release that is not yet confirmed (example: about $14 to $23 on a buyout headline, then straight back through $15 when it proved false).
Entry: no entry on extreme news until the dust settles; treat press releases with skepticism.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: the real risk is a full round trip to pre-news levels.
Time: any.
Refuse if: buying and holding a catalyst spike on an unconfirmed headline; all-in bets on a "next GameStop" claim or fake FDA news.
