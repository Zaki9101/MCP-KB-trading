Conditions: Price hits the stop or exit set before entry, or a trade goes against you.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: sell at once and look for the next setup. A stop is a loss, not a mistake; the mistake is moving it or holding past max loss. Moves inside the planned range are no reason to change the plan.
Target and exits: a trade that does not work immediately: take the small loss. Before holding a loser, ask whether you would still hold it if its cost were already written off; if not, close it.
Time: any.
Refuse if: adding, averaging down or calling it an "investment" (a $200 stop with adds became $1,000+); blaming market makers or manipulation.
