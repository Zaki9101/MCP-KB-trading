Conditions: Count consecutive losing trades in the session.
Entry: between the first and third loser take only plan-compliant PlayBook setups; never double size on the fourth.
Stop: three consecutive full-risk losers = stop for the session. A loser counts unless it is a scratch (a loss under 25% of the per-trade max loss).
Target and exits: close positions and stop for the day.
Time: whole session.
Refuse if: any tilt sign after a loser; continuing after the third non-scratch loser.
