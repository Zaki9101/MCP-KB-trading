Conditions: If direction is not clear, assume a trading range. Equal-distance odds sit near 50-50 most of the day; a strong trend reaches 60-70% only briefly. If no trend started in the first hour or two, assume a range day. A trend day can change: accept it.
Entry: long momentum entries only on a clearly trending breakout or a with-trend pullback (range-edge fades are not in this library).
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: every breakout is followed by a trading range; most reversals become ranges.
Time: any.
Refuse if: entering in the middle of a range; outside bars inside a range; a tight range with large bars (breakouts often fail).
