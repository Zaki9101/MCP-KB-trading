Conditions: Raise size only if ALL hold: 7 of the last 10 days positive and net positive at the current size; at most +20% of size per 10 positive days; 2-3 consecutive green weeks; a monthly raise needs 17 of 20 days gross-positive. A second trade per day needs 5-6 green weeks. A calendar month alone is never a gate.
Entry: size up only on A+ setups, only inside the 25% ceiling.
Stop: while losing, no added size and no added stop width.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: review at the end of each day and week.
Refuse if: sizing up after losses or to recover; raising tier to escape a slump.
