Conditions: A stock you want to "keep an eye on".
Entry: no feelers. Not a PlayBook trigger = watch it. A trigger = planned size. Exception: 100 shares as the starter size of a defined setup with a stop.
Stop: every position has a stop.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: a position that only commits you psychologically; feelers in stocks you do not watch closely (feelers and scratches pushed one win rate to about 40%).
