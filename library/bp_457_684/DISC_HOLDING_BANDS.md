Conditions: Monthly, split results by cents per share held: .01-.09, .10-.24, .25+. Example month: .01-.09 netted -$670.49 while .10-.24 and .25+ made the money.
Entry: slice stats by shares per trade, price band, hour, weekday and setup; cut what nets negative; press the best setups.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: hold 10c+ trades to their Reason2Sell.
Time: monthly review.
Refuse if: your .01-.09 band nets zero or negative: cut scratches and feelers (they only cost fees and attention).
