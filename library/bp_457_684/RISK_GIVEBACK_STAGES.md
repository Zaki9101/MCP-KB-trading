Conditions: Track the day's peak P&L. The lines are dormant until the day's profit passes your "profitable open" threshold ($500-$1,000 in the books; scale it).
Entry: after stage 1, half size and A+ only.
Stop: stage 1 at 15% of peak profit given back = 10-minute break, then half size and A+ only. Stage 2 at 30% = close everything and stop for the day. Never give back 50%. Midday: 30% of the Open's profit; a breach lowers the next day's Open tier.
Target and exits: a dollar goal is never a reason to stop; the giveback line always is.
Time: whole session.
Refuse if: stage 2 has fired.
