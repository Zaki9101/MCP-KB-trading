Conditions: Choose scalp or swing before entry and write the stop and first target.
Entry: handbook floor: reward at least 2.0 x risk. A scalp target needs price to trade one tick past it. A scalp with a stop larger than its target needs about a 70% win rate (break-even 66.7% at 2 risk for 1 reward). Swings usually win 50% or less and must pay about 2:1.
Stop: inside the cent caps.
Target and exits: do not change the plan once in the trade; many losses come from managing a scalp as a swing, or the reverse.
Time: any.
Refuse if: scalping without a measured win rate above break-even (default to swing; scalping is for experienced traders at Tier1, Ch.19).
