Conditions: Price rises but volume does not; asks climb while the bid stays static or flat.
Entry: do not buy: it is a long trap that often ends in a flush through stops.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: in a long rally, volume drifting down gradually is an early caution flag even while the trend holds.
Time: any.
Refuse if: rising price on only average or low volume (weak uptrend, low participation); ask prices climbing over a flat bid.
