Conditions: An open small-account pullback trade.
Entry: not stated.
Stop: not stated (the hard stop stays live).
Target and exits: exit if any one appears: the MACD line crosses the MACD signal line; volume decreases; a "Jackknife Rejection" appears. Jackknife is never defined in the source: treat it as a sharp rejection candle against the position.
Time: while the trade is open.
Refuse if: do not trade a numeric Jackknife trigger; none is stated.
