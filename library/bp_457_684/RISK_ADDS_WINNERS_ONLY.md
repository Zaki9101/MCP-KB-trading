Conditions: Add only to a position already working.
Entry: every add has its own stop; total risk of all lots stays inside the tier (Brooks: total risk equals one normal trade); each add no larger than the previous lot unless the stops on all lots sit at breakeven or better. Scale-ins only in the first two-thirds of the day. Under $5, buy on the bid only when tape and Level 2 show a held bid.
Stop: automatic stops on all lots when holding 3 or more lots; trail them; never cover early by hand.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: first two-thirds of the session.
Refuse if: adding to a loser or averaging down; open profit used to risk more than the plan.
