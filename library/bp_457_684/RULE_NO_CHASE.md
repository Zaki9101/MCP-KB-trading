Conditions: The stock is already moving without you.
Entry: wait for price action at the real level, then go above it. Missed trigger: chase at most 3c (5c only after logged results at 3c; Ch.19). No good trade = no trade.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: any.
Refuse if: the stock already made the first 10-20c; you missed a breakout (buying above the range out of frustration is the real mistake; missed moves are not losses); a vertical move with no structure (wait for another base).
