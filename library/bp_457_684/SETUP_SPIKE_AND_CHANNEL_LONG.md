Conditions: A spike of one or more strong bull bars, then a pause of about 1-10 bars that becomes a shallower channel with overlapping bars and at least 3 pushes. A pause longer than 10 bars is a trading range, not a channel.
Entry: trade with the trend inside the channel: buy above the bounce bar at a double bottom flag in the channel.
Stop: not stated (Ch.19 cent caps).
Target and exits: measured move from the open of the first spike bar to the high or close of the last spike bar, projected from the channel start. The channel usually ends with a test of its bottom: do not hold the long into that test.
Time: main window.
Refuse if: calling every channel a trend when it is retracing; scaling into the position against you (forbidden). A channel break against the trend usually fails within about five bars.
