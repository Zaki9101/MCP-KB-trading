Conditions: About to enter.
Entry: rate how much of the urge is the setup versus fear of missing out; record the FOMO % as a number in the journal.
Stop: not stated.
Target and exits: not stated.
Time: before every entry.
Refuse if: FOMO over 50%; buying because others are making money in the stock; chasing a missed move above the range (opportunity cost only); the day's hottest stock you cannot read ("No" is a fine answer to "did you trade it?").
