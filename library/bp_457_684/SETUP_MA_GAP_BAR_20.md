Conditions: Strong bull trend day; 20 or more consecutive bars never touch the 20-bar EMA (over two hours in the example); a countertrend move has failed and trend bars resume.
Entry: the first touch of the MA after the gap run is a with-trend entry; editor trigger: one tick above the gap bar high. No scaling in lower (never add to a loser, Ch.19). Tier1 first entry.
Stop: editor: below the gap bar low, inside the cent cap.
Target and exits: a new high of the day, often running into the close; flat before the close.
Time: book: after about 14:30 ET. Ch.19: after 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size, with your own hour-of-day edge.
Refuse if: no MA gap of 2+ hours; the countertrend move becomes a channel break with a strong bar against the trend.
