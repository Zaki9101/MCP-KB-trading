Conditions: Holding a long; write the list before entry.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: Sell or lighten on: target reached; intraday trend broken on your timeframe; breaking news in the stock or market; market at important resistance; unusual or massive seller; the buying pattern dissipates; time clock; move too steep or pullback too deep; day ends; vertical spike on a volume jump (sell at least half); price spikes but volume falls at resistance; spreads widen and bids thin (take half before target).
Time: Any; the bell can be the exit.
Refuse if: Selling with no Reason2Sell present just because the position shows a profit.
