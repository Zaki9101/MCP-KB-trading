Conditions: All times US Eastern; every new long.
Entry: No new entries 9:20-9:35 (watch only). From 9:35 only a named open pattern at Tier1; open-drive entries only until about 10:00. Main window 9:35-11:00 (11:30 only for a stock still moving clean). No new entries 11:00-13:00. After 13:00 only proven setups at reduced size with your own hour-of-day edge.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: Climax and drying-volume exits apply at any time. Flat before the close.
Time: Stop trading at 11:00 or two hours from the first entry, whichever is first.
Refuse if: Beginners sit out the first minutes; late or unprepared = no open trading.
