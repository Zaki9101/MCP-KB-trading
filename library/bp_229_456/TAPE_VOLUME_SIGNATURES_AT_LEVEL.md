Conditions: Reading volume against price at a level. Signatures mark levels and exit zones; they are not entries by themselves.
Entry: Passive accumulation (heavy volume, price not falling at a low) needs a trigger from another setup. Aggressive accumulation (slow steady rise on steady volume) is the stage to hold.
Stop: Support breaks after passive accumulation = close all longs.
Target and exits: Heavy volume with no progress at resistance = unload longs. Peak volume with price strength on a breakout = reason to exit. Slow selling into support usually continues lower; fast selling with a vertical bar makes a base likelier.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Buying the break when volume was already strong at resistance and spikes on a vertical move (exhausted); buying support with no trigger.
