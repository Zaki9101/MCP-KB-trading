Conditions: Count your own attempts at a level or stock today, not the market's touches.
Entry: Second try only on a new confirmed signal after the setup rebuilds, with a fresh stop, at most half the first try's size and dollar risk. A third market test of a base you never traded = first entry at Tier1; a flipped level = new level at Tier1.
Stop: Fresh stop on every try.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Per day.
Refuse if: A third try of your own; two stop-outs at the level or in the stock (drop it for the day); price below the original hold level; small-cap runner after the first failed re-entry.
