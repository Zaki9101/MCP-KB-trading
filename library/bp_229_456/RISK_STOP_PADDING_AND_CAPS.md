Conditions: Every day-trade stop.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: At the structure that proves the idea wrong. Pad 1-3c past an ordinary level, 5-10c past a whole or half number or obvious range edge. Caps: 10c on $1-$8 stocks, 20c on any stock until the size-up gates, about 30c after them at the same dollar risk. 2-5c only when a visible held bid or offer is the stop.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Padding pushes the stop beyond the cap (skip the trade); tightening into the noise to make it fit.
