Conditions: Before the session: mark prior-day high/low, premarket high/low, previous close, whole and half dollars, VWAP and moving averages.
Entry: Write each level with its if-then plan before price reaches it; set alerts 1c beyond the level. Live chart: three lines only (nearest level in your direction, your stop level, next opposing level).
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: At the desk 7:30-8:00; premarket support/resistance about 9:00; final list 9:20; watch the last 10 minutes before the open.
Refuse if: Deciding how to trade a level in the middle of the trade; no if-then plan; late or unprepared (no open trading).
