Conditions: The day's profit has passed the 'profitable open' threshold ($500-$1,000 in the books; scale it). Measure on marked P&L, closed plus open.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: At 15% given back from the day's peak: 10-minute break, then half size and A+ setups only. At 30%: close everything and stop for the day. 50% is rejected as a limit.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Continuing to trade after a 30% giveback.
