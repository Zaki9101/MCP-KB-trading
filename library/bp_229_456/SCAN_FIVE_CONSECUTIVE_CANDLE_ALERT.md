Conditions: Scanner flags 5 or more consecutive same-direction 5-min candles with elevated relative volume (examples: rel vol about 1.3-1.9, 15-min volume 115-227%; WETF rel vol 1.90-2.58).
Entry: Alert only: put the name on screen and watch the 1-min chart. The trigger is the New High After a Down-Streak card.
Stop: Not stated.
Target and exits: Not stated.
Time: Whenever the scanner fires; entries still follow the Ch. 19 clock.
Refuse if: Trading the count alone without 1-min confirmation; a 4-candle reading is an alert, not a setup.
