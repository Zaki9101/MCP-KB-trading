Conditions: Uptrend: stair-step bases with a volume spike on each break, or a rising channel. First entry comes from another setup; this card governs adds.
Entry: Only on structure: buy the base low or the break of the base high, within your risk. Never the vertical bar.
Stop: About 5c under the newest consolidation low; channel: under the lower line, or under the upper line once price goes vertical. inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Optional partial per step; sell into the vertical stage. Exit on loss of support, failure at the latest resistance, or a step under 50% or over 75% of the prior step's duration.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: No base (vertical bar); leg 3+ without strong Level 2; hectic action.
