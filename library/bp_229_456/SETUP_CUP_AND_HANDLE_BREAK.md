Conditions: Rounded cup in a market uptrend, best near the day's high; volume repeats the bowl, dries in the handle, rises on the break. Cup at least 30 min; handle at most 50% of cup depth and 30% of cup time.
Entry: Buy the rim break after the handle with the ask thinning, or the held rim retest. Tier1. Never inside the base.
Stop: Below the handle low, inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: No fixed target. Half into the first volume spike, stop to breakeven, trail the rest. Exit on a price-plus-volume peak or if the broken rim fails.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00. Can work mid-day.
Refuse if: Handle or cup limits broken; volume does not repeat the shape; market not uptrending; index against you.
