Conditions: Mature up-move. Euphoria = near-vertical bar on the largest volume bar of the move; or a slow advance on shrinking volume. A steady climb on steady volume is trend: hold. No book gives a number for 'vertical' or 'largest'; compare with the last 20-30 bars.
Entry: No long entry in a vertical spike.
Stop: Held long: normal trailing stop; after a vertical break out of an up-channel, just below the upper channel line.
Target and exits: Sell fully or partly into the spike bar; trending market: partial and trail; choppy: exit fully. Drying volume: sell into the fade, do not wait for the top to confirm. Missed it: sell into the 20 MA retrace.
Time: Any time.
Refuse if: Buying the parabolic stage after missing the move; treating euphoria as a stand-alone short.
