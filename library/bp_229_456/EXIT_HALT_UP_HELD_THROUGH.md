Conditions: Already long when a surging stock halts up (grey halt levels on Level 2, ask pinned to the top). First or second halt of the day only.
Entry: No new entry; the position is held through the halt.
Stop: Not given by the source; a halt can freeze your stop. Hold only a size you could carry if it reopened 20% against you (editor note); reduced size and no margin on halt-prone stocks.
Target and exits: Sell 50% on strength after the resume; the rest at the next important level; close the rest at once if a level such as the 200 MA sits just above.
Time: The resume minute and the first candles after it.
Refuse if: Third or later halt; halt down; price breaks K after the resume.
