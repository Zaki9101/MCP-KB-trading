Conditions: Stock washes out below today's open, bottoms, and volume rises as price moves back toward the open. It should resolve quickly once triggered.
Entry: Buy the first 1-min or 5-min candle that makes a new high after the washout as price turns green versus the open.
Stop: Low of day only if it lies inside the Ch. 19 cap from entry; otherwise stop at the cap under the reclaim candle's low, or skip. Resting order for any size above Tier1.
Target and exits: Book: half at about 1:1, then breakeven. Ch. 19: half at 1R only on a defined tape signal; otherwise 25-50% at 2.0R.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: False reclaim that rolls back red; no volume behind the move; repeated dips in a choppy name.
