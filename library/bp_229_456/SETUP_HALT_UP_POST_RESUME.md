Conditions: Reopen higher than the halt level after the first or second halt up. K = reopen (first candle low), T = first candle high, L = forming candle high, R = second candle low. R holds, tape fast, Level 2 buyers present.
Entry: Default: buy the T break. T-L micro-pullback entry only when R holds and buyers are clearly present.
Stop: T-break: the L area just under T. T-L: 1-2c below entry (book); Ch. 19 allows 2-5c only with a held bid as the stop. Price in K-R: exit at once.
Target and exits: Book scalps 5-6c on 1-2c risk. Hidden seller (heavy prints, no progress) = exit.
Time: First 1-3 minutes after the resume. Halts last 5, 10 or 20 min; assume at least 5 min with no exit.
Refuse if: Third or later halt; halt down; K broken; tiring buyers; slow platform; beginner on the staircase.
