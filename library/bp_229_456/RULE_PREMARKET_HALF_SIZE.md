Conditions: Premarket trading (the book's clock 8:30-9:30; Ch. 19 small-account row 7:00-9:20).
Entry: Half size or less (CEP: one half to one quarter), even on clean fast movers.
Stop: inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Before 9:20; nothing 9:20-9:35.
Refuse if: Expecting halts to protect you (no halts premarket); unclean spreads; interns (no premarket).
