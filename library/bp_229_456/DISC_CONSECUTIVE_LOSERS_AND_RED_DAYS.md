Conditions: Losing trades or days in a row.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: 3 full-risk losers = stop for the session (scratches under 25% of the per-trade max do not count). 2 red days: basics only. 3 red days: review that evening, Tier1 only until every trade is reviewed. 5-7 struggle days or a slump: half size for a week.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Raising the tier to escape a slump.
