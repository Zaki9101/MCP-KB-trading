Conditions: Extended stock surging with few pullbacks. A leg = one push up plus a small pullback; a 5-min consolidation resets the 1-min count to a new leg 1.
Entry: Legs 1-2: standard Level 2-confirmed entries; on an extended stock the only 5-min entry is the break of the previous candle's high. Legs 3-4: only with strong Level 2, reduced size, first target taken fast. Beyond leg 4: no trade (editor note).
Stop: inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: On legs 3-4 take the first target fast.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Entry on the vertical bar instead of a base; leg 3 or later without strong Level 2; any leg beyond 4.
