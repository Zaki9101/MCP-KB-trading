Conditions: A long position at a level that starts to break.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Write the exit trigger before entry: the stop price, the held bid decrementing, or the offer holding below the level. Wait at most 3c or the hard stop, whichever comes first.
Target and exits: Once you decide to exit, take the easier price; the stop price is mechanical, the exit price is a calm judgment.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: No waiting at all if an aggressive seller or news was visible before the break.
