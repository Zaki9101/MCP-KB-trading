Conditions: Any open long, any setup.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Breakout level, consolidation low, base low or confidence level lost = out.
Target and exits: Exit now if: price returns to entry and the idea is not working; topping tail or flush candle at a new HOD, or a doji after 5-10 green candles; an ask wall or important MA (200 MA) just above; a vertical bar with a volume spike at resistance (scale out); price spikes as volume drops, or volume dries while price makes new highs; give-back reaches 15% of peak profit (break, half size) or 30% (stop).
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Letting one missed move change your exit discipline.
