Conditions: Every trade, every day.
Entry: Never add to a loser. Never widen a stop. Never double size after losses. Never trade past the daily stop. Buying a pullback in a trend is a new trade, not averaging down; buying more because it went lower is.
Stop: Never moved against you once set.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Doubling down: the book's test won about 85% of the time but the 15% losses outweighed the gains.
