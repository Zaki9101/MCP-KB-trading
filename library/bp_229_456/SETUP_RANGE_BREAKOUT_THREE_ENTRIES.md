Conditions: Range with a clear top and bottom, each touched repeatedly; volume rises on pushes, dries on pullbacks; orderly creep to the trigger; stock stronger than the index.
Entry: Default regular (buy the break with tape confirmation) or conservative (first strength after a held retest of the broken top). Aggressive, just under the trigger, only per the entry-method card at Tier1.
Stop: Aggressive and regular: below the range low; conservative: just under the retested level. inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Next opposing level with room of at least 5x risk.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: Sharp run into the trigger; vertical volume spike at resistance with no base; hard long-term resistance a few multiples of risk away; index sharply against you.
