Conditions: Position already working: level holds, bids step up, the seller lifts, SPY confirms.
Entry: Add only on confirmation. Each add no larger than the previous lot, unless the stop on all lots already guarantees breakeven or better. Pre-planned lots are allowed only if planned before lot 1, each has its own stop and the trigger is a confirmation, not a better price.
Stop: Every add gets its own stop; total risk of all lots stays inside the tier budget.
Target and exits: If the extra shares do not act at once, lighten.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Position below entry or already stopped; reward too small; tape not clean; you cannot hold or exit the extra size at the same reward:risk.
