Conditions: Before every click.
Entry: Know the stop price and type, the dollar risk of all lots, the first target with reward:risk of 2.0 or better, your Reasons2Sell, the order type and maximum price. SPY not at major resistance for a long; no news due; you can exit all of it at the same reward:risk.
Stop: Set before entry where the reason for the trade is gone, then size from it. No stop number = no trade. No single trade may stop you out for the day.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Any checklist item missing.
