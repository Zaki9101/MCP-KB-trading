Conditions: Several red candles or a big pullback, then a hammer (lower wick longer than the small body) on a volume burst. Capitulation needs a vertical drop AND accelerating volume.
Entry: Break of the hammer high AND a Level 2 signal. If not broken within 2-3 minutes, the entry moves to the HOD break. A higher pivot low after the hammer is preferred.
Stop: Below the hammer or vertical-drop low; any new low after entry = out. inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Not given; capitulation may be only a scalp. Ch. 19 2.0R first-target minimum.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: No Level 2 signal even with a perfect chart; no preceding downtrend; capitulation missing either component; hammer high not broken.
