Conditions: Opening range breakout. Low-float stocks are not ORB candidates; use the bull-flag or gap-and-go sheets for them.
Entry: 5-min opening range, entry no earlier than 9:35, Tier1. Mid and large caps may use the 15-min range (entry 9:45) or 30-min range (entry 10:00).
Stop: Opposite side of the range, or a close back through VWAP, whichever comes first, inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Not stated on these pages.
Time: From 9:35.
Refuse if: A 1-min ORB (it enters inside the 9:20-9:35 no-entry window); a low-float name.
