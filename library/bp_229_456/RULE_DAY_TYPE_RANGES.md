Conditions: Name the day type before choosing setups: trend, regular range, narrow range, expanding range.
Entry: Trend day: with-trend breakouts and pullbacks. Narrow range: no trading inside it, wait for the break. A tight consolidation (a few cents for more than 15-20 minutes) with no jump and no volume signature: no trade inside it or on its first break.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: Not applicable.
Time: Any; cut activity on range days.
Refuse if: Expanding range (higher highs and lower lows alternate, each break fails): stand aside.
