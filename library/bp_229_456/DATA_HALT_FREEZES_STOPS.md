Conditions: Fast, large move with high relative volume, especially with no visible news (no-news squeezes can halt for 'unusual market activity').
Entry: Decide before any halt whether you will hold through an unknown reopen at all.
Stop: A resting stop does NOT execute during a halt; the reopen can continue or reverse hard. Assume at least 5 minutes with no exit, much longer on news.
Target and exits: Reduced size and no margin on halt-prone stocks.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Holding size you could not carry through a gap against you; not knowing your broker's halt and stop handling (verify with your broker).
