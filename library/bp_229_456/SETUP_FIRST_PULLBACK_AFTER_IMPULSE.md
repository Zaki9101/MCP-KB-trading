Conditions: Long flat overnight or premarket base, then a sharp green impulse candle out of it (a red-to-green cross of the prior close or premarket reference near the open).
Entry: Buy the first shallow 1-2 candle pullback right after the impulse once it holds above the breakout level; or the candle that crosses back above the reference.
Stop: Not numbered; below the pullback or reclaim candle's low (editor note), inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Not numbered; ride the new trend. Examples exit near the first spike high; the second leg often fades.
Time: Near the open; Ch. 19 entries from 9:35.
Refuse if: Pullback re-enters the prior base range; not the first pullback; no volume on the impulse.
