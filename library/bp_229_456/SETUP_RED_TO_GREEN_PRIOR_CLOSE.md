Conditions: Stock trading red moves toward the previous day's close on high volume.
Entry: Buy as price approaches the prior close on high volume; the prior close is the target.
Stop: The nearest technical level used for entry: VWAP break if bought near VWAP, MA or support break if bought there; inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Sell all at the prior close, or move the stop to breakeven and let it run if still working. Single exit only if the prior close is at least 2.0R away; otherwise half size with a logged 60%+ win rate, or skip.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00.
Refuse if: No high volume; the move does not work immediately; target under 2.0R without the logged record.
