Conditions: Holding a long and reading Level 2 and Time and Sales.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Not applicable.
Target and exits: Act at once on: a big bid eaten with accelerating red prints; heavy prints at one price with no price progress (hidden seller); a wall on the ask (close everything); price cannot break a level (exit at breakeven, no re-entry there until it breaks); your lower offers get no takers; SPY turns and the tape sells; a hectic zone after the peak with no base (scale out or sell all).
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Re-entering at a level the stock just failed to break.
