Conditions: Stock gaps up above the highest high of the last 20 trading days on good news (hard filter).
Entry: Wait 15 minutes after the open; place a limit buy at the close of the first 15-min candle. If unfilled within 15 minutes, cancel; never chase.
Stop: Exit if price trades below the first 15 minutes' low; that distance must still fit inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Book: hold, exit one minute before the close or around 12:00-1:00pm ET. Ch. 19 governs: be out by the 11:00 cutoff or two hours after entry, whichever is first.
Time: Entry about 9:45 (limit window 15 minutes).
Refuse if: No 20-day-high gap; no identifiable good news; order unfilled in the window.
