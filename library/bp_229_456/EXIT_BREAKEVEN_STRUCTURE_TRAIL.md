Conditions: First partial sold or first target reached.
Entry: Not applicable.
Stop: Move to breakeven, then raise by hand to the newest structure: step low, channel line, last higher low, or the 9 EMA on the chart you traded (update every 5-10 min on the 5-min). Keep a resting stop at the current stop price.
Target and exits: Trail only under a level with real support; not so close that normal noise takes you out. New traders: no automatic trailing-stop orders.
Time: Any.
Refuse if: Trailing under a level that shows no support; moving the stop on a few cents of noise.
