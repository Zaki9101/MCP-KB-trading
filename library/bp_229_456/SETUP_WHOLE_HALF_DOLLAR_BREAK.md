Conditions: Uptrending stock approaches a whole or half dollar from below on rising volume (a HOD scanner hit adds confidence); volume done at the price, bid not dropping.
Entry: Buy the first candle that breaks and holds the .00 or .50. The $X.99 anticipation entry is aggressive: Tier1 only.
Stop: Flat 10c (notes), inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: If already long into the level, scale out in the $X.90s and keep the rest at breakeven. Next whole or half dollar as target.
Time: Ch. 19 clock: entries 9:35-11:00 (11:30 if still clean); none 9:20-9:35 or 11:00-13:00. A level can chop for hours; a late break needs fresh volume.
Refuse if: Break runs then reverses through a second, steeper trendline without holding (fake-out); first-candle chase before the hold; bare round bid with no volume.
