Conditions: A clear premarket high (variant A), or a lower premarket pivot reached by a stair-step decline (variant B).
Entry: Buy the candle that closes above the premarket high or pivot, with follow-through green candles and rising volume. Tier1, as a named open pattern.
Stop: Breakout candle low or the broken level, padded 1-3c (editor note), inside the Ch. 19 cap (10c on $1-$8, 20c otherwise before the size-up gates; wider = skip).
Target and exits: Next opposing level with at least 2.0R; trail under rising 1-min higher lows. Examples fade fast: sell into the first spike or volume climax.
Time: Book: at or just after the 9:30 open. Ch. 19: entries from 9:35 only.
Refuse if: No clean premarket high or pivot (choppy, no stair-step); a poke without a close and hold; price fades back into the premarket range.
